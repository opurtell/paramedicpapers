  • Tabletop MCI exercises: how they're run and measured

    Interactive Journal of Medical Research · 6 Oct 2026 · Scoping review · Context only

    Scoping review of 13 studies from 9 countries on tabletop exercises for prehospital mass casualty preparedness — it built a preliminary three-tier 'TTX Design Spectrum' typology and classified outcomes with the Kirkpatrick model (plus an applied-learning level), finding evaluation focused on participant learning with less assessment of applied performance or system outcomes.

Education & training research

21 recent papers on education & training in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • TeamSTEPPS training shows no measurable gain

    BMC Emergency Medicine · 17 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    A Norwegian TeamSTEPPS programme for ambulance staff measured teamwork and safety-culture perceptions at baseline, 4 and 12 months — and found no statistically significant change in any dimension despite the structured training.

  • Situational judgement tests in paramedic hiring

    Cureus · 15 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    National survey of 21 Canadian paramedic services found situational judgement tests and multiple mini-interviews are rarely used in frontline paramedic hiring, mostly by smaller services despite the tools being designed for mass testing.

  • AR training keeps teen AED skills fresh

    Health Education Research · 15 Oct 2026 · Other · Context only

    Quasi-experimental study of 193 secondary students found all three AED teaching methods produced safe, effective shocks initially, but the augmented-reality group retained a 100% effective shock rate at 3 months while the traditionally trained group declined significantly.

  • Paramedics feel weakest in neonatal and paediatric mental health

    Acta Paediatrica (Oslo, Norway · 10 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    A survey of 253 Finnish paramedic graduates found moderate self-rated paediatric competence (mean 2.92/5) but low neonatal competence (2.04), with paediatric mental health emergencies rated lowest (1.30) and limited simulation or placement opportunities reported.

  • Greek national school CPR championship

    Resuscitation Plus · 27 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Description and evaluation of Greece's first national school CPR championship (Kids Save Lives) — 196 schools and 432 students competed with QCPR-measured BLS skills, scoring 95.7–96.3% on average (88.2% in special-needs schools), and 99% of the 197 surveyed participants endorsed it as effective training worth repeating.

  • CONOCES-BLS scale adapted for citizens

    Resuscitation Plus · 20 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Spanish methodological study adapting the CONOCES-BLS knowledge scale for adult laypersons via Delphi expert consensus and pilot testing — content validity was strong (expert agreement above 0.90 on every item, Krippendorff coefficients 0.91–1.0) and internal consistency adequate (Cronbach's alpha 0.82), supporting its use for assessing community BLS knowledge pending further psychometric work.

  • Knowledge gaps in local anaesthetic dosing and LAST

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 15 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    Multicentre survey of 114 emergency clinicians across three Queensland EDs — fewer than half correctly identified the recommended maximum dose for any of four local anaesthetics, 21.9% couldn't recall any features of LAST, and only 10.5% could state the lipid emulsion dosing regimen.

  • Kigali mass casualty training lifts knowledge

    African Journal of Emergency Medicine · 11 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental pre–post study of a two-part mass casualty training curriculum (asynchronous video modules plus a half-day simulation) for 41 emergency providers and community first responders in Kigali — mean knowledge scores rose significantly after training and remained above baseline at 3- and 6-month follow-up, with significant confidence gains across key MCI skills.

  • VR adds little to CPR simulation training

    Nurse Education Today · 10 Sep 2026 · RCT · Context only

    RCT of 246 undergraduate nursing students testing immersive VR plus manikin simulation against manikin simulation alone for CPR group training — no significant difference in self-confidence or CPR group performance after cluster adjustment, though students rated VR higher on satisfaction and usability, and 33.6% reported dizziness and 19% headaches from headsets.

  • Big shift to online EMS continuing education

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 25 Aug 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective analysis of 19.3 million CE completions showed post-pandemic shift to online formats, with asynchronous distributive learning increasing 209% and interactive gaming courses increasing 915%.

  • Medical students can teach infant BLS

    Resuscitation Plus · 20 Aug 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental study testing whether physician-supervised medical students can deliver infant BLS training to caregivers — knowledge scores improved significantly after training and were sustained at 12 months, with no difference between physician and student-led groups.

  • Simulation builds team confidence for prone/beach-chair arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · 27 Jul 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    A half-day, multi-station in situ simulation course at an elective surgical unit was associated with higher self-reported confidence at three months, and self-reported application of course content to real patients.

  • ChatGPT-4o generates neonatal resus scenarios

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 26 May 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    This prospective multicenter pilot tested whether ChatGPT-4o and DeepSeek-R1 could generate valid neonatal resuscitation simulation scenarios for training, finding ChatGPT-4o performed comparably to official NRP scenarios in overall quality while demonstrating strengths in debriefing design but having gaps in dynamic vital sign provision.

  • Practical education boosts mental health confidence

    International Emergency Nursing · 23 May 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    They tested whether practical-based education improved paramedic students' confidence and competence in mental health assessment and management, and found hands-on training significantly improved both self-efficacy and clinical performance compared with traditional classroom teaching.

  • EM resident physician response vehicle exposure

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 12 May 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Five-year retrospective of a physician response vehicle staffed by EM residents found residents gained valuable exposure to adult and paediatric cardiac arrests, refusals, death pronouncements, and critical trauma, with an average of 28-79 encounters per resident.

  • Professionalism as core EMS competency statement

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 11 May 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    This study validated a situational judgement test for selecting paramedic students, finding it effectively assessed non-technical skills and predicted suitability for paramedic training.

Paediatrics research

34 recent papers on paediatrics in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Age-adjusted MAP centiles for sick kids

    Archives of Disease in Childhood · 8 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Large US observational study deriving and validating age-specific mean arterial pressure centile curves in prehospital, ED and critical-care datasets — lower age-adjusted MAP tracked with mortality and greater vasoactive and fluid use, though the curves fitted neonates in the EMS sample poorly.

  • Anaphylaxis knowledge gaps in prehospital crews

    Iranian Journal of Allergy, Asthma, and Immunology · 4 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Cross-sectional survey of 322 prehospital personnel found only about half could identify all three paediatric anaphylaxis diagnostic criteria or use an adrenaline auto-injector correctly, though 82% recognised adrenaline as first-line treatment.

  • Nearly half of paediatric doses off-protocol

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 3 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    US quality-registry cohort of 386 prehospital paediatric medication administrations found 46.4% deviated at least 20% from protocolised dosing — mostly underdoses — with ondansetron (aOR 36.9) and the intranasal route (aOR 7.3) the biggest drivers.

  • EMS and ED disagree on paediatric asthma signs

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 3 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Prospective study of 295 children transported with asthma exacerbations found only slight-to-fair agreement between EMS and ED clinicians on asthma severity exam findings, suggesting a prehospital paediatric asthma severity score would be hard to validate.

  • Paediatric non-transport re-call rates low

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 29 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    US retrospective study of 1.85 million paediatric ambulance calls (40% not transported) — only 1% of non-transported children re-called and were transported within 72 hours and deaths were rare (0.1% dead on scene at re-call), with under-5s, neurologic impressions and disadvantaged areas over-represented among re-calls.

  • Two-handed neonatal mask ventilation training

    Resuscitation Plus · 28 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Before-and-after simulation study of a respiratory-function-monitor-guided two-handed neonatal PPV training package in 40 clinicians — the two-handed hold cut median facemask leak to 2.5% vs 27% one-handed before training, leak was low with both holds after training, and preference for two-handed ventilation rose from 27% to 50%.

  • Paediatric in-hospital arrest trends in Japan

    Resuscitation · 27 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Nationwide Japanese administrative data (2011–2022, 7.1M paediatric admissions) testing whether paediatric in-hospital arrest incidence and outcomes are changing — incidence fell from 1.03 to 0.86 per 1000 admissions while survival to discharge rose from 25.8% to 29.6%, with adrenaline given in 71% and intubation in 62% of arrests.

  • Post-ROSC PaCO2 doesn't predict paediatric OHCA outcome

    Resuscitation · 25 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Japanese multicentre registry study of 215 paediatric OHCA patients testing whether the first post-ROSC PaCO2 category predicts 30-day outcomes — no clear association with favourable neurologic outcome or survival for either hypocapnia (aOR 0.83) or hypercapnia (aOR 1.46), though estimates were imprecise with wide confidence intervals.

  • Paediatric air medical intubation performance

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    US national cohort of 1,040 paediatric air medical intubations — 86.5% first-pass success overall but only 69.1% in infants under 1, with ketamine the commonest induction agent and substantial site-to-site variation

  • Intranasal dexmedetomidine paediatric sedation

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Townsville cohort of 114 children given intranasal dexmedetomidine for ED procedural sedation — effective as a sole agent in 78%, adverse events in 13% (mostly mild bradycardia), but ED stays ran long at a median 395 minutes

  • Paediatric IV ketamine sedation very safe

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 19 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Systematic review of 20 studies covering 67,871 children having IV ketamine procedural sedation in EDs — no deaths or permanent adverse outcomes, sentinel events pooled at about 1 in 11,558 sedations, serious adverse events 0.34%, with age ≥10, upper respiratory infection and opioid co-administration modest predictors while ASA status and benzodiazepine co-administration were not.

  • Anonymous video deletion biased newborn RCT

    Resuscitation · 18 Sep 2026 · Other · Context only

    Norwegian analysis of the NeoBeat RCT testing whether anonymous staff deletion of resuscitation videos introduced selection bias — after the anonymous opt-out, enrolled newborns had significantly higher Apgar scores, shorter ventilation and fewer admissions, and the trial was terminated halfway without power to conclude on its outcomes.

  • Delphi consensus on paediatric procedural sedation

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 15 Sep 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Two-round Delphi consensus of 28 paediatric emergency clinicians from 23 Spanish centres — strong agreement on monitoring standards, staffing and safety requirements for paediatric procedural sedation, with ketamine the preferred agent for painful procedures.

  • Paediatric weight often undocumented before dosing

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 12 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Analysis of 57,378 US paediatric 9-1-1 responses where weight-based medications were given found 73.1% had a documented weight (or length-based estimate), with documentation less likely for adolescents (OR 0.87), Black patients (OR 0.72) and lower-acuity cases (OR 0.59), and more likely with advanced life support crews (OR 2.02).

  • Bronchiolitis discharge criteria cut length of stay

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 4 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Systematic review for the binational guideline update on discharge criteria for infants under 12 months with bronchiolitis — two retrospective studies (N=2,697) found structured discharge criteria significantly shortened length of stay with no difference in readmissions (low-to-very-low certainty), and informed the 2025 Australasian Bronchiolitis Guideline covering clinical stability, oxygen saturation/support, feeding, caregiver confidence, social factors and follow-up.

  • Paediatric trauma diagnosis accuracy in Swiss HEMS

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 29 Aug 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Multi-centre Swiss HEMS study of 282 paediatric trauma patients found moderate diagnostic accuracy between prehospital and in-hospital diagnoses (Cohen's κ=0.64), with polytrauma underdiagnosed and extremity/pelvic injuries overdiagnosed, and overall survival of 92.2%.

  • Community paramedics for paediatric concussion follow-up

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 29 Aug 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Experimental mixed-methods study demonstrated that a Mobile Integrated Health program using community paramedics for paediatric concussion follow-up was feasible and acceptable, with 19% patient enrolment and improvement in Post-Concussion Symptoms Scale scores.

  • Adrenaline given in only 60% of paediatric OHCA

    Resuscitation Plus · 29 Aug 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Retrospective analysis of US NEMSIS data found adrenaline was administered in only 60% of paediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrests, with longer scene times, advanced airway attempts, and defibrillation associated with higher odds of administration, while public settings and female sex correlated with lower odds.

  • Paediatric teleconsultation during ambulance transport

    Resuscitation Plus · 28 Aug 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Simulated RCT testing teleconsultation for paediatric ambulance transports — paramedics found it acceptable (TUS 5.8/7) and experienced less frustration (p=0.03) with 94% attempting physician contact, though physicians reported higher workload (p=0.01) and 92% connected in ≤2 attempts.

  • AR decision support grabs clinician gaze

    Resuscitation Plus · 18 Aug 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Secondary analysis of a pediatric cardiac arrest simulation trial found AR-based decision support captured 42-59% of visual attention with distinct role-specific fixation patterns among team leaders and medication nurses

  • Singapore paediatric OHCA incidence and survival

    Resuscitation Plus · 10 Aug 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Abstract not yet available (ahead-of-print). Observational study reporting incidence, aetiologies and survival outcomes of paediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in Singapore.

  • US paediatric ED use: low EMS share, high acuity

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 6 Aug 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Retrospective analysis of 202.5 million US pediatric ED visits (2016–2022) found children comprised only 7% of EMS transports but presented with higher acuity, more abnormal vital signs, and increasing psychiatric and behavioral presentations over time.

  • Tactile stimulation site in neonatal resuscitation

    Resuscitation · 27 Jul 2026 · RCT · Supports current practice

    There was no difference in 5-min SpO2, need for further resuscitation in DR, cerebral oxygenation, and in-hospital complications with the three modes of TS, sole rub, trunk rub and back rub.

  • Poor field differentiation of infant upper vs lower airway disease

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    EMS clinicians documented similar primary impressions and administered many of the same treatments across both upper and lower airway groups, demonstrating minimal differentiation in prehospital management of young children.

  • Synchronised ventilation faster ROSC in neonatal piglets

    Resuscitation Plus · 27 Jul 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    In a neonatal piglet model of asystolic cardiac arrest, CCSV resulted in a significantly faster time to ROSC compared to 3:1 C:V.

  • Observation cuts CT in paediatric abdominal trauma

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Supports current practice

    Observation with deferred CT decisionmaking was associated with less frequent CT use, particularly in patients with intermediate clinician suspicion, without an increase in missed injuries.

  • Prehospital physicians underconfident in paediatrics

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 31 May 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    They surveyed emergency physicians and analysed 9,409 protocols to understand pediatric emergency preparedness, and found pediatric cases represent only 6.5% of prehospital physician deployments but physicians reported low confidence in neonatal care, intoxication, and pediatric polytrauma.

  • IM beats intranasal midazolam in paediatric seizures

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 6 May 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Meta-analysis testing whether intranasal or intramuscular midazolam works better for paediatric seizures without cannula access — intramuscular midazolam was associated with faster seizure termination and less need for rescue therapy, especially in the prehospital setting.

Other research

15 recent papers on other in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Cluster trials read differently to individual ones

    Critical Care Medicine · 24 Oct 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Meta-epidemiological study of 65 meta-analyses (212 trials, 315,276 patients) comparing cluster-randomised with individually randomised trials in prehospital, emergency and critical care — no difference in mortality effect estimates, but cluster trials reported significantly smaller effect sizes for length-of-stay-type outcomes.

  • Prehospital ultrasound as a decision tool

    The Ultrasound Journal · 24 Oct 2026 · Other · Context only

    Structured narrative review of 38 articles on prehospital POCUS across cardiac arrest, shock, dyspnoea, trauma, paediatric and obstetric uses — it can identify reversible causes in arrest, characterise shock and support trauma triage and destination decisions, but limited sensitivity means it can't rule anything out.

  • AU/NZ emergency care research priorities being set

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 23 Oct 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    ACEM's Clinical Trials Network, with the James Lind Alliance, has launched a priority setting partnership to set the emergency care research agenda for Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand for the next 5–10 years.

  • Emergency Department Operational Measures Associated with EMS Redirection Activation and Duration

    Western Journal of Emergency Medicine · 20 Oct 2026

    Single-centre New York study over 392 days found EMS ambulance redirection activated on 63.8% of days, with ED visit volume associated with activation and door-to-room time associated with both activation and cumulative duration — but boarding and admissions were not.

  • EMS response time and patient outcomes in high-acuity emergency responses: a Danish matched cohort study

    International Journal of Emergency Medicine · 20 Oct 2026

    Danish matched cohort of 171,708 highest-priority (A-level) responses in Region Zealand tested whether EMS response times affect outcomes — mortality did not rise with longer response times, and the longest-quartile group had lower 30-day (OR 0.87) and 2-day (OR 0.82) mortality than the shortest quartiles.

  • Challenges of turning prehospital prediction of trauma-induced coagulopathy into early administration of tranexamic acid

    Trauma Surgery & Acute Care Open · 20 Oct 2026

    Editorial using a prehospital machine-learning risk model for trauma-induced coagulopathy found medium- and higher-risk patients still frequently miss tranexamic acid within the 1-hour window, arguing better risk stratification should close the evidence-to-practice gap.

  • Establishing a regional prehospital whole blood program in a border community: the Trauma Regional Advisory Council-V initiative in the Rio Grande Valley of Texas

    Trauma Surgery & Acute Care Open · 20 Oct 2026

    Implementation report describing how four Texas border counties built a regional prehospital whole blood program across eight domains — governance, MOUs, rotation systems, provider credentialing and grant-funded equipment for rural agencies.

  • Race, Gender, and Survival: a Scoping Review of Disparities in ICU Post-Resuscitation Care and Neurological Outcomes Following Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest

    Journal of Racial and Ethnic Health Disparities · 20 Oct 2026

    Scoping review of 15 studies found Black women in the US received less bystander CPR, slower emergency response, less ICU intervention and more early DNR/withdrawal decisions after OHCA, with the disparities compounding across the chain of survival.

  • Paramedics strained by end-of-life care gaps

    Healthcare · 17 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    A UK survey of 182 paramedics found end-of-life care in the community is undermined by practical gaps — 54% often had no access to the medicines they needed, 88% couldn't reach advance care planning documentation, and only 28% felt competent in the role.

  • How to write medicolegal statements

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 22 Sep 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Practical guide on the statement types Australian emergency clinicians may be asked to provide for police, coroners and criminal proceedings — they're statements of fact rather than opinion, and structure and clarity matter

  • Single-dose aminoglycoside AKI risk in CKD

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 4 Aug 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective cohort across four Australian hospitals — single-dose aminoglycosides caused AKI in 30.9% of CKD patients vs 13.4% without CKD (OR 2.88)

  • Chronic liver disease ED presentations in Queensland

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    CLD‐related ED presentations are increasing and place substantial demand on hospital services in Queensland.

  • Prehospital NEWS predicts death despite low ED triage

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Prehospital physiologic instability was associated with increased in-hospital mortality among patients classified as low risk at ED triage.

  • Wearable Doppler ultrasound for prehospital monitoring

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 27 May 2026 · Other · Context only

    They reviewed wearable Doppler ultrasound technology for prehospital haemodynamic monitoring, and found it may detect occult haemodynamic compromise earlier than standard vital signs alone.

  • GPT estimates pneumothorax size to guide care

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 22 Apr 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Tested whether a vision-enabled GPT model could estimate pneumothorax size and guide tube thoracostomy vs conservative care, and it matched radiologist measurements closely with good agreement to real-world ED decisions.

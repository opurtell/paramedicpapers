Airway & ventilation research

20 recent papers on airway & ventilation in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Prehospital RSI: what makes it hard

    Health SA = SA Gesondheid · 7 Oct 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Qualitative focus-group study of 39 South African emergency care practitioners found pre-hospital RSI is hampered by system-driven administrative demands, resource and workforce constraints, and communication gaps affecting clinical decision-making.

  • NRB + nasal cannula inferior to flush-rate preoxygenation

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 29 Sep 2026 · Other · Supports current practice

    Healthy-volunteer crossover study — NRB plus nasal cannula at 15–25 L/min each produced expired oxygen of 68–75% versus 85% for flush-rate NRB, so the portable-cylinder workaround is inferior when supply allows flush rate.

  • Feedback improves neonatal face mask ventilation

    Resuscitation Plus · 26 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Context only

    Prospective simulation study in neonatology fellows testing low-dose, high-frequency face mask ventilation practice with real-time respiratory function monitor feedback across T-piece, self-inflating and flow-inflating bags — feedback lifted tidal volumes in target range from 62.3% to 80.3% and cut mask leak on every device, while extra sessions without feedback added little.

  • SGA vs intubation in OHCA: ROSC lower with SGA

    Resuscitation Plus · 25 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Hypothesis-generating

    Systematic review and meta-analysis comparing supraglottic airway devices with endotracheal intubation in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest. Abstract not yet available (ahead-of-print).

  • VAPOX preoxygenation in ED intubation

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Australian retrospective cohort of 116 patients intubated with ventilator-assisted preoxygenation (VAPOX) in two EDs — 78% achieved first-pass success without hypoxaemia, but half had at least one complication and 36% became hypotensive after intubation

  • Ventilation feedback devices inaccurate during CPR

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Context only

    Simulation cross-over study of two commercial ventilation feedback devices used during continuous compressions with asynchronous ventilations — both were clinically inaccurate, one displaying 111 breaths/min when the true rate was 12 and underestimating tidal volume by up to 440 mL in reversed airflow.

  • Prehospital cricothyroidotomy: rare but often successful

    Resuscitation · 3 Sep 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Nationwide Danish cohort of all 71 prehospital emergency cricothyroidotomies over 2016–2023 — overall procedural success 87.3% (83.3% in OHCA), aspiration and oropharyngeal bleeding the commonest indications, but the procedure was rare (0.12% of 39,885 OHCAs) and OHCA outcomes were poor (ROSC 25%, 30-day survival 4.5%, 1-year survival 2.3%).

  • ED intubation success high with videolaryngoscopy

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 25 Aug 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective study of 1,250 ED intubations showed 93.8% first-pass success with videolaryngoscopy as primary technique, with hypotension and desaturation as most common adverse events.

  • Capnometry with prehospital intubation cuts mortality

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 20 Aug 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Nationwide registry study examining capnometry use during prehospital intubation and its association with 30-day mortality.

  • Predictors of hyperoxaemia after prehospital oxygen

    The American Journal of Emergency Medicine · 16 Aug 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Prospective cohort of 245 patients found 24.5% had hyperoxemia (PaO₂ >120 mmHg), with intubation (OR 38.89) and transport duration ≥24 min (OR 13.74) as independent predictors.

  • High adverse event rate after prehospital RSI

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 4 Aug 2026 · Cross-sectional · Supports current practice

    Investigates the prevalence of adverse events following prehospital airway management using neuromuscular blockade

  • Mechanical vs manual ventilation in CPR equal

    Resuscitation · 3 Aug 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    This systematic review of 13 articles (six RCTs, seven non-randomised studies) found very low to low certainty evidence showing no significant difference in ROSC between volume-controlled mechanical ventilation and manual ventilation during CPR.

  • Miller vs Macintosh blade in paediatric intubation

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 27 Jul 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    In this cohort of young children intubated in the ED, Macintosh blade use was associated with higher FPS and fewer hypoxic events compared with Miller blade.

  • Awake intubation by emergency physicians feasible

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 27 Jul 2026 · Other · Context only

    In this preliminary single-center study, emergency physicians performed awake intubation with relatively high incidence, high overall success, and low rates of major adverse events.

  • Hypopharyngeal perforation after King laryngeal tube

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 27 Jul 2026 · Case series · Context only

    Hypopharyngeal perforation is a rare but serious complication of prehospital King laryngeal tube placement.

  • Bolus-only prehospital RSI maintenance highly variable

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 25 Jun 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective study showing that prehospital anaesthesia maintenance using intermittent boluses results in high variability of cumulative sedative/analgesic doses, potentially risking sub-therapeutic plasma levels.

  • AI in prehospital airway care still experimental

    International Journal of Medical Informatics · 23 May 2026 · Systematic review · Hypothesis-generating

    They reviewed AI applications in prehospital airway management and found most tools remain experimental, with limited integration into clinical workflows despite promising capabilities for difficult airway prediction and real-time guidance.

  • Alfen blind intubation device works on manikins

    BMJ Innovations · 18 May 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Manikin study with 157 intubation attempts achieved >98% success rate with the novel blind intubation device across operators of varying experience.

  • Italian HEMS airway equipment varies, training patchy

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 14 May 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    This national survey documented airway management skills and device availability across Italian HEMS services, showing variability in advanced airway training and device adoption.

  • Automated ventilation wins in helicopter short-haul

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 6 May 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Simulation study comparing bag-valve ventilation with automated ventilation during short-haul helicopter rescue using an i-gel — automated ventilation achieved target minute ventilation more consistently, caused less airway movement, and avoided disconnects.

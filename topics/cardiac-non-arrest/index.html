Cardiac (non-arrest) research

8 recent papers on cardiac (non-arrest) in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Aortic emergencies: HR/BP drugs in transport

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Cohort of 1,530 aortic emergencies in US critical care transport — esmolol, labetalol and nicardipine strategies hit HR/BP targets at similar rates, with esmolol alone showing the highest hypotension incidence (9.3%) and labetalol alone the lowest (2.1%)

  • 48-hour AF cardioversion threshold abandoned

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 22 Sep 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    EMA commentary arguing the once-conventional 48-hour elective cardioversion threshold for recent-onset atrial fibrillation is no longer supported

  • LLMs unreliable for cath-lab activation ECGs

    Heart & Lung · 17 Sep 2026 · Other · Context only

    Retrospective test of three multimodal LLMs against real cardiology cath-lab activation decisions on 615 prehospital ECGs found the best (Gemini, 95.3% sensitivity) had 9.4% specificity, and the standard ECG machine algorithm beat all three LLMs overall — general-purpose LLMs aren't reliable for STEMI pathway activation.

  • Only a third get PHT within 30 min

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 30 Jul 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    This Australian study identifies factors affecting timely prehospital thrombolysis delivery in Victoria's pharmacoinvasive STEMI network.

  • FLAG score predicts circulatory support need in shock

    Heart & Lung · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    The FLAG risk score is a simple, registry-based model to predict MCS treatment during hospital stay in a prehospital setting.

  • SCARE score validates for chest pain triage

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 4 Jun 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    They prospectively validated the SCARE score for predicting acute myocardial infarction during telephone triage across multiple French emergency communication centers, finding good discrimination and calibration but noting limitations in real-world implementation.

  • EMS protocols rarely guide pads in STEMI

    The American Journal of Emergency Medicine · 23 May 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    They reviewed whether EMS protocols adequately address defibrillator pad placement in prehospital STEMI care, and found significant variability and gaps that may compromise monitoring and pacing capability during transport.

  • PANDA trial: noradrenaline vs adrenaline in shock

    American Heart Journal · 23 May 2026 · RCT · Context only

    This multicenter RCT protocol tests whether noradrenaline improves outcomes compared with adrenaline for paramedic management of cardiogenic shock, with the PANDA trial aiming to settle the debate on optimal vasopressor choice in the prehospital setting.

Respiratory research

3 recent papers on respiratory in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • HFNO reduces intubation vs conventional oxygen

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 15 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 RCTs (1,955 patients) comparing high-flow nasal oxygen with conventional oxygen and NIV for acute respiratory failure in the ED — HFNO reduced intubation versus conventional oxygen (RR 0.61) but not versus NIV, with no clear benefit over either in COPD or acute heart failure.

  • Poor agreement on radiological tension features

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 27 Jul 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    Interpretation for radiological tension features had poor inter‐rater reliability, which seriously changes their appropriateness as drivers for interventional management.

  • One dose dexamethasone enough for paediatric asthma

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Supports current practice

    Nearly two-thirds of children with asthma exacerbations were treated with 2 doses of dexamethasone rather than a single dose, even though treatment with 2 doses was not associated with improved outcomes.

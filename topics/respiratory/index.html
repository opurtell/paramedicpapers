Respiratory research

  • Breathlessness self-management linked to fewer ambulance calls

    BMC Pulmonary Medicine · 18 Nov 2026 · Other · Context only

    An Australian co-designed feasibility study of a home-based breathlessness intervention service found 140 completers over 22 months, with 90% reporting improvement in daily activities and 21% reporting they had avoided calling an ambulance for breathlessness.

  • Harder ambulance transports, higher pneumonia mortality

    Acute Medicine & Surgery · 17 Nov 2026 · Registry · Context only

    A Japanese population registry study of 78,720 patients transported for suspected bacterial pneumonia in Osaka found difficult ambulance transport (four or more hospital selections and 30+ minutes on scene) rose from 2.7% pre-pandemic to 10.3% during COVID-19 and was associated with higher in-hospital mortality (adjusted OR 1.14).

  • Camera-based breathing rate in moving ambulances

    Diagnostics · 13 Oct 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Japanese researchers built a contact-free respiratory-rate monitor using RGB and thermal cameras that worked inside a moving ambulance, with errors of 0.59–4.38 breaths/min depending on posture and respiratory pattern.

  • HFNO reduces intubation vs conventional oxygen

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 15 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of 17 RCTs (1,955 patients) comparing high-flow nasal oxygen with conventional oxygen and NIV for acute respiratory failure in the ED — HFNO reduced intubation versus conventional oxygen (RR 0.61) but not versus NIV, with no clear benefit over either in COPD or acute heart failure.

  • Poor agreement on radiological tension features

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 27 Jul 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    Interpretation for radiological tension features had poor inter‐rater reliability, which seriously changes their appropriateness as drivers for interventional management.

  • One dose dexamethasone enough for paediatric asthma

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 27 Jul 2026 · Cohort · Supports current practice

    Nearly two-thirds of children with asthma exacerbations were treated with 2 doses of dexamethasone rather than a single dose, even though treatment with 2 doses was not associated with improved outcomes.

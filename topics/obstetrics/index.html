Obstetrics research

8 recent papers on obstetrics in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Ambulance CTG streamed to hospital during maternal transfer

    PloS One · 1 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    A Japanese feasibility study transferred 68 pregnant women from nine primary obstetric clinics to a university hospital with real-time remote CTG monitoring from an ambulance, achieving legible fetal heart-rate traces in all transfers (median 91.1% legible recording time) with no sessions lost to connectivity issues over median transport of 34.5 minutes.

  • Severe hypertension in pregnancy rarely treated to guideline

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 31 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A US national EMS dataset study of 39,546 responses for pregnant patients beyond 20 weeks found persistent severe hypertension in 3.1%, of whom only 0.8% received guideline-adherent treatment with both magnesium and an antihypertensive.

  • How Utah widened paramedic oxytocin scope

    Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health · 24 Oct 2026 · Other · Context only

    Quality improvement initiative describing how a rural US state changed its EMS protocols in April 2024 to let paramedics give oxytocin to prevent postpartum haemorrhage and AEMTs give it intramuscularly for prevention and treatment.

  • Hypothermia common after unplanned out-of-hospital birth

    Acta Obstetricia Et Gynecologica Scandinavica · 17 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Supports current practice

    A companion French registry analysis of 3,473 unplanned out-of-hospital births found maternal complications in 8.4% and perinatal complications in 4.4%, with preterm birth and neonatal hypothermia — which affected 63% of newborns — the main risk factors.

  • How rare is a prehospital birth? Very

    Acta Anaesthesiologica Scandinavica · 6 Oct 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Eight-year cross-sectional review of Danish EMS records found unplanned prehospital births attended by ambulance staff are rare — 310 assisted deliveries, 0.03% of all missions — with most complications manageable by crews.

  • Guideline updates changed prehospital birth care

    Emergencias · 22 Sep 2026 · Registry · Context only

    A 13-year French registry of 3,473 unplanned out-of-hospital births attended by 79 EMS centres found guideline dissemination drove practice change — more prophylactic oxytocin, less routine suctioning — but response times lengthened and neonatal thermal protection worsened.

  • RhD-positive prehospital blood OK for women

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 2 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Narrative review synthesising evidence on D-alloimmunization and HDFN risk found modeling studies estimate fetal death due to anti-D-mediated HDFN would occur in fewer than 0.1% of females of childbearing potential exposed to RhD-positive blood products in trauma, supporting prehospital RhD-positive transfusion when RhD-negative unavailable.

  • Low HDFN risk after prehospital RhD-positive blood

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 1 Sep 2026 · Modelling · Supports current practice

    In silico simulation modelling future haemolytic disease of the fetus and newborn risk in 957 US females of childbearing potential who received prehospital RhD-positive blood — annual HDFN risk ranged 0.214–0.294% across regions

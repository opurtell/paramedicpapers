Cardiac arrest research

124 recent papers on cardiac arrest in paramedic and prehospital care, newest first. Each has a plain-English summary, the study design, the main finding and caveat, and what it means for Australian practice.

  • Family CPR helps — but strangers' CPR helps more

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Oct 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    US CARES registry cohort of 824,960 OHCAs comparing family-member vs non-family bystander CPR — both beat no bystander CPR, but bystanders outside the family achieved notably higher survival.

  • Defibrillation strategy RCT gets underway

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Oct 2026 · RCT · Context only

    Protocol for a multinational 3-arm RCT testing whether anterior-posterior pad placement or double sequential defibrillation beats standard anterior-lateral defibrillation for 30-day survival in shockable OHCA — no results yet, with 320 of a planned 909 patients enrolled.

  • Only a third of arrests get bystander CPR

    Heart, Lung & Circulation · 8 Oct 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Systematic review and meta-analysis of 229 studies quantifying global bystander CPR rates in OHCA — just under a third of patients get bystander CPR, with arrests at home 28% less likely to receive it than public-location arrests.

  • Amiodarone after non-shockable-to-shockable conversion

    Resuscitation · 8 Oct 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    German Resuscitation Registry propensity-matched cohort testing whether amiodarone helps OHCA patients whose initial non-shockable rhythm converts to shockable — it was associated with better 24-hour and survival-to-discharge, though not clearly better neurological outcome.

  • Long resuscitations don't rule out good recovery

    Resuscitation · 8 Oct 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Danish national registry study of bystander-witnessed OHCA survivors asking whether longer time-to-ROSC means worse long-term outcomes — anoxic brain damage rose with time, but long-term survival, return to work and nursing-home admission did not, showing prolonged resuscitation doesn't preclude good recovery.

  • Bystander AEDs work — when someone actually grabs one

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 8 Oct 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Korean multicentre OHCA registry analysis estimating bystander AED effectiveness against a no-defibrillation reference — AED application was associated with outcomes comparable to or better than EMS shocks, but was applied in only a fraction of eligible cases, especially at home.

  • Cancer markedly raises OHCA risk

    JACC. Advances · 7 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Swedish matched cohort of ~800,000 cancer patients and 3.7 million controls found cancer carried a 55% higher hazard of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (aHR 1.55), with the greatest short-term excess in lung and neurological cancers.

  • Paediatric OHCA: half had warning symptoms

    Open Heart · 6 Oct 2026 · Case series · Practice prompt

    UK single-centre review of 58 consecutive paediatric out-of-hospital VF arrest survivors without a pre-existing diagnosis found 50.9% had reported cardiac symptoms beforehand — syncope in 23.6%, half the arrests during exertion — and of 13 children with prior syncope only three had sought medical attention and none had follow-up arranged, pointing to missed opportunities to diagnose inherited cardiac conditions before arrest.

  • What actually degrades CPR performance

    BMC Emergency Medicine · 5 Oct 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Qualitative interviews with 21 resuscitation team members in Kermanshah, Iran building a model of how environmental threats, cognitive and motivational barriers, psychological stressors and socio-cultural factors interact to compromise CPR performance.

  • What OHCA care is worth paying for

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 3 Oct 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Systematic review of 10 economic evaluations of prehospital OHCA care found early defibrillation and EMT-delivered ALS, prehospital ECPR, and termination-of-resuscitation rules are all cost-effective, while health-equity reporting across the studies was poor.

  • Early adrenaline + early airway in OHCA

    The Journal of Emergency Medicine · 2 Oct 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    Korean nationwide registry cohort of 20,662 OHCA patients testing whether early adrenaline (≤10 min) combined with early advanced airway management (≤6 min) improves outcomes — the combination roughly doubled odds of good neurological recovery (aOR 2.44) and survival to discharge (aOR 2.18) versus late/late, and early adrenaline with late airway showed no benefit, but the interaction attenuated in sensitivity analyses addressing resuscitation time bias, so it is hypothesis-generating rather than confirmatory.

  • PIHCA vs GO-FAR 2 for IHCA prediction

    Resuscitation Plus · 30 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    External validation of the PIHCA and GO-FAR 2 pre-arrest scores across 501 Swedish in-hospital cardiac arrests (28% survived with favourable neurological outcome) found comparable discrimination for favourable outcome (AUROC 0.73 vs 0.70, difference not significant) and adequate calibration for both scores.

  • No sex differences in OHCA with confirmed CAD

    Resuscitation Plus · 29 Sep 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Post hoc analysis of a prospective European OHCA registry (2012–2023) testing whether sex-based differences in care persist when obstructive CAD is angiographically confirmed as the arrest cause — among 580 patients (15.3% women), PCI use, procedural success, targeted temperature management and ECPR were similar between sexes, 30-day survival was 66.2% in women vs 61.5% in men (p=0.33), favourable neurological outcome 55.1% vs 53.8% (p=0.82), and female sex was not independently associated with 30-day survival (adjusted OR 0.75, not significant).

  • Post-OHCA outcomes at Victorian Heart Hospital

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 29 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Audit of 121 post-arrest patients at the Victorian Heart Hospital — 50.4% survived to discharge with 92.6% prehospital ROSC and 61.2% shockable rhythms, and 24% needed interhospital transfer, mostly for non-cardiac causes.

  • Pit-crew CPR training improves intact survival

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 26 Sep 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Report of an EMS pit-crew CPR educational initiative associated with improved neurologically intact survival after out-of-hospital cardiac arrest — abstract not yet available (ahead-of-print), so the study design and effect size can't be verified yet.

  • No evidence on screening arrest survivors' psychology

    Resuscitation · 26 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Systematic review of structured identification and assessment of psychological, emotional or cognitive problems in cardiac arrest survivors — of 1,605 records screened, none met eligibility, so there is no evidence base for which screening instruments, thresholds, timing or referral pathways should be used.

  • Higher CPR SpO2 linked to ROSC, not good outcome

    Resuscitation · 25 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Study of 98 OHCAs asking whether pulse oximetry readings during CPR relate to outcomes — higher event-average SpO2 was associated with sustained ROSC (aOR 1.05, AUC 0.77, cutpoint ~71%) and survival to admission, but not neurological intact survival, and cycle-average SpO2 did not track compression quality metrics.

  • Drone AED network modelling for Scotland

    Reliability Engineering & System Safety · 25 Sep 2026 · Modelling · Hypothesis-generating

    Bayesian optimisation of a drone-assisted AED delivery network using geographically referenced Scottish cardiac arrest data — station placement driven by OHCA survival probability and existing EMS coverage, with cost-effectiveness analysis suggesting drone AED delivery is cost-effective and could meaningfully improve response coverage in rural areas with long ambulance response times.

  • rCAST score in young children after arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · 25 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    External validation of the rCAST post-cardiac arrest prognostic score in 75 infants and young children after targeted temperature management — good discrimination (AUC 0.947 for unfavourable neurologic outcome, 0.857 for mortality) with no favourable outcomes in the high-severity group, but 76% of low-score patients did well and the authors caution against using it for clinical decisions.

  • Dispatcher CPR barriers delay bystander CPR, cut survival

    Resuscitation · 24 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective cohort of 3,263 transcribed Swedish 112 calls linked to the national cardiac arrest registry quantified barriers to dispatcher-assisted CPR after the dispatcher recognised arrest — 43.7% of calls hit at least one barrier (commonest: inability to move the patient), bystander CPR reached 85% in barrier-free calls but stayed below 50% with three or more, and any barrier was associated with lower ROSC (adjusted RR 0.89) and 30-day survival (adjusted RR 0.57).

  • Night drone AED delivery feasible but workload high

    Resuscitation Plus · 24 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental mixed-methods study of 24 human-supervised drone flights delivering a 480 g CellAED over 877 m in a semi-urban area — all flights completed without safety incidents and night flight times matched day (mean ~140 s vs ~136 s, p=0.43), though pilots reported markedly higher cognitive workload and environmental complexity after dark.

  • Post-arrest biomarker platforms don't agree

    Resuscitation Plus · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Platform-comparison study of post-arrest HIBI biomarkers (GFAP, Nf-L, tau, UCH-L1) — lab-grade analyser results correlated highly with each other, but the point-of-care i-STAT agreed poorly, so prognostication thresholds can't be shared across devices

  • Smartphone CPR app: ROSC up, survival not

    Resuscitation Plus · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Before–after study of 615 OHCA patients after the MySOS smartphone bystander-CPR app launched in Bangkok — post-implementation ROSC probability rose ~6 percentage points, but 24-hour and discharge survival showed no significant improvement

  • Bystander CPR benefits greatest in deprived areas

    Resuscitation Plus · 22 Sep 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Analysis of 184,727 Korean OHCA cases found bystander CPR rates were lowest in the most deprived regions, yet its association with good neurological outcome was strongest there (aOR 1.56 vs 1.31–1.34 in better-off regions)

  • In-hospital arrests in resource-limited Somalia

    Resuscitation Plus · 22 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Prospective study of 240 in-hospital arrests in a Mogadishu ED — ROSC 5.8%, survival past 24 hours 1.3%, with CPR initiation documented in only 15% of cases, underlining system gaps rather than treatment questions

  • Multi-domain post-arrest prognostication data feasibility

    Resuscitation Plus · 20 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Canadian prospective feasibility study testing whether multi-domain prognostication data (biomarkers, transcranial Doppler, EEG, neuroimaging, six-month outcomes) can be collected in 30 adults after cardiac arrest — day-1 biomarkers (97%) and CT/MRI were feasible, but serial transcranial Doppler (77%), day-7 biomarker sampling (71%) and the complete six-month multidomain battery (33%) fell short, with remote or proxy-assisted follow-up improving outcome ascertainment.

  • DNAR orders missed in OHCA dispatches

    Resuscitation · 19 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    One-year regional study of 1,695 lights-and-sirens dispatches for suspected OHCA in Southern Denmark — 15.7% involved a patient with a physician-issued hospital DNAR order that dispatchers and crews couldn't access, one-day mortality was 90.6% in that group, and the authors call for DNAR orders to be visible to emergency dispatch and prehospital providers.

  • Transport destination not linked to OHCA outcomes

    Resuscitation Plus · 19 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Population-based cohort of 12,663 OHCA transports in Nagoya under a dispatch-guided destination system — transport to critical care medical centres showed no association with favourable one-month neurological outcomes overall, was linked to lower one-month survival in non-shockable rhythms (OR 0.66), and only refractory shockable rhythms showed a possible survival edge (19.7% vs 3.3%, imprecise estimate).

  • DNACPR termination policies poorly applied in Japan

    Resuscitation Plus · 19 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Nationwide Japanese survey of 561 fire departments — 47% had a prehospital termination-of-resuscitation policy for OHCA with DNACPR wishes, yet 52% of departments with evaluable data applied it to zero cases during 2024 despite encountering eligible patients, and departments with a policy transported far fewer arrests with ongoing CPR (46% vs 82%).

  • CPR-induced consciousness rising in OHCA

    Resuscitation · 18 Sep 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    Ambulance Victoria registry study of 43,402 adult OHCA cases (2008–2024) identifying CPR-induced consciousness — 1,438 patients (3.3%), with annual incidence rising (IRR 1.083, 95% CI 1.07–1.10) across all arrest rhythms, and CPRIC associated with improved survival to hospital discharge.

  • Best height for standing compressions

    Resuscitation Plus · 17 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Simulation study with 30 participants compressing at seven working heights found knee-height bed or stretcher position gave the best compressions (52.7 mm mean depth, 80.6% correct depth), with positions 15–20 cm above knee height significantly worse.

  • Mild hypercapnia during ECPR in pigs

    Resuscitation Plus · 16 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Porcine refractory-VF arrest model (n=12) testing mild hypercapnia (PaCO2 50-55 mmHg) during ECPR — the hypercapnia group showed higher cerebral glucose (p=0.01) and lower caspase-8 expression, but no significant difference in brain tissue oxygen tension (49.9 vs 36.4 mmHg, p=0.13), intracranial pressure, EEG activity or serum brain-injury biomarkers.

  • CPR quality maintained across paramedic-to-ED handover

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 15 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Observational study of 35 OHCA transfers tracking CPR quality across the paramedic-to-ED handover — chest compression fraction (89–92%) and rate stayed guideline-compliant in every interval with no deterioration at transition, though compression depth was significantly deeper during paramedic intervals.

  • Few OHCA patients eligible for eCPR in Germany

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 10 Sep 2026 · Modelling · Context only

    Ten-year German registry study modelling eCPR eligibility after OHCA in a mixed urban-rural region — of 1,696 analysed arrests, only 348 (20.5%) met local eligibility criteria at ambulance arrival and just 149 (8.8%) met the stricter modified ELSO definition, with the study also testing whether hypothetical intra-arrest transport strategies could meet a 60-minute low-flow target.

  • Relatives' needs after failed OHCA resuscitation

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 8 Sep 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Interviews with 15 relatives present at unsuccessful out-of-hospital resuscitations showed their needs shift across three phases — dispatch guidance, help-or-withdraw during resus, then compassionate unhurried communication when efforts stop.

  • Most ECPR-eligible London OHCAs can't reach ECMO in time

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Geospatial analysis of five years of London Ambulance Service OHCA data — of 776 patients meeting ECPR eligibility criteria, only 3% could reach an ECMO-capable centre within the 45-minute target, and stretching it to 60 minutes would lift access by 217%.

  • Women post-OHCA get less angiography

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Sep 2026 · Registry · Context only

    Nationwide Swedish cohort of 8,641 post-OHCA admissions — women were less likely to undergo coronary angiography and PCI than men, yet intervention rates were even once angiographic findings were accounted for, and a third of angiographed women without shockable rhythm or ST elevation had significant coronary disease.

  • Double-click metronome preserves CPR quality

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Secondary analysis of 477 volunteers in prolonged manikin CPR — compression depth and chest recoil decayed within minutes, and a double-click metronome with separate compression and release cues preserved quality significantly longer than a standard metronome.

  • Community CPR training only helps public arrests

    Resuscitation · 3 Sep 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    Difference-in-differences evaluation of a year-long community hands-only CPR training initiative across 20,414 OHCAs — overall bystander CPR rates rose from 37.4% to 42.5% but the adjusted intervention effect wasn't significant (+2.94 pp, 95% CI −0.95 to 6.83); only public-location arrests showed a significant increase (+8.59 pp), with no effect at home and no detectable survival or neurological benefit.

  • Intra-arrest TOE feasible but no survival gain

    Resuscitation · 3 Sep 2026 · Systematic review · Hypothesis-generating

    Scoping review of 22 studies (1,254 patients, 84% OHCA) mapping intra-arrest transoesophageal echocardiography — insertion succeeded in 80–100% of cases and TEE changed management in 31–48% of patients, but the two randomised trials found no difference in ROSC, survival, or favourable neurological outcome versus conventional CPR.

  • Racial gaps in bystander CPR for opioid OHCA

  • Paramedic elevation doesn't help CPR in obesity simulation

  • Reversible causes: ED vs out-of-hospital arrest

  • Lactate after paediatric OHCA predicts outcome

Daily scan

Paramedic research, 28 November 2026

A quiet day leaning cardiac: a Belgian physiology study found reversed airflow (air forced back out of the lungs during compressions) in 83% of intubated OHCA patients on transport ventilators, and a Canadian cohort described coagulopathy in trauma patients after prehospital transfusion.

  • Reversed airflow common during OHCA mechanical ventilation

    Resuscitation · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    A Belgian physician-staffed team recorded intratracheal pressures in 12 intubated adults with out-of-hospital cardiac arrest during continuous compressions and volume-controlled ventilation, finding reversed airflow in 10 of 12 patients and 82% of ventilations, median 61 mL per episode.

  • Coagulopathy common after prehospital transfusion in long transports

    BMC Emergency Medicine · Cohort · Context only

    A Canadian retrospective cohort of 70 trauma patients transfused by Ornge air ambulance found 16% with INR > 1.5 and 20% with fibrinogen < 1.5 g/L at trauma centre arrival, with coagulopathy associated with higher mortality and massive haemorrhage protocol activation.

  • Electronic prehospital records far more complete than paper

    International Journal of Emergency Medicine · Cohort · Supports current practice

    A German retrospective cohort of 2,781 prehospital physician reports for suspected ACS found electronic documentation reached 90.9% record completeness versus 48.2% for paper (aOR 10.48 for a complete record).

  • Non-transport response vehicles match stroke code times

    Emergencias · Cohort · Context only

    A Spanish retrospective cohort of 1,263 stroke codes attended by SUMMA-112 in 2022 found rapid response vehicles matched mobile intensive care units on call-to-assessment times (median 27 minutes both), assessment-to-door times (44 vs 46 minutes) and reperfusion rates.

  • Venue factors rarely explain public AED use

    Resuscitation Plus · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A Taiwanese registry study with field visits to 76 public venues in Taichung found 34.2% had documented AED use before EMS arrival, and although used venues had more AEDs per site, security staff and foot traffic, no venue-level factor remained significant after multivariable adjustment.

  • What drives first responder mental health after trauma

    Frontiers in Psychology · Systematic review · Context only

    A scoping review of 14 studies (2017–2024) mapped psychological outcomes and interventions for first responders after disaster deployments and cumulative occupational trauma, finding exposure intensity, social isolation, maladaptive coping and lack of preparatory training associated with poorer outcomes.

  • Nursing triage score for prehospital trauma in China

    BMC Emergency Medicine · Cohort · Context only

    A Chinese retrospective cohort of 2,864 prehospital trauma patients developed and internally validated a five-variable nursing triage score (GCS, SBP < 90 mmHg, active haemorrhage, fatal trauma type, age ≥ 65) for 24-hour mortality, ISS ≥ 16 or emergent surgery, with AUROC 0.883 in the validation set.

  • Smartwatch cardiac alerts feasible but poorly worn

    Acta Cardiologica Sinica · Other · Context only

    A Taiwanese feasibility study of 96 participants (75 high-risk cardiovascular patients) tested smartwatch photoplethysmography alerts triggering EMS dispatch or case-manager follow-up, with 3 of 16 critical alerts leading to EMS dispatch and median wearing time of only 0.7 days in the high-risk group.

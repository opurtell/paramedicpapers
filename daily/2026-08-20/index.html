Paramedic research, 20 August 2026

5 new paramedic and prehospital papers from the 20 August 2026 scan.

  • Intranasal equals subcutaneous ketamine for pain

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · RCT · Supports current practice

    RCT testing whether intranasal ketamine is non-inferior to subcutaneous for acute traumatic pain — both routes provided equivalent analgesia with minimal clinically important differences, though minor adverse events were more common with subcutaneous administration.

  • Trauma undertriage still high in Tromsø

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Registry · Context only

    Retrospective registry study assessing trauma undertriage using ISS ≥16 and NISS ≥16 as reference — found 15% undertriage (above the recommended <5%), with 63% of undertriaged patients meeting national criteria but not receiving trauma team activation, mostly low-energy falls.

  • Capnometry with prehospital intubation cuts mortality

    Prehospital Emergency Care · Registry · Supports current practice

    Nationwide registry study examining capnometry use during prehospital intubation and its association with 30-day mortality.

  • Mapping medical clearance of psychiatric ED patients

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · Systematic review · Context only

    Scoping review mapping medical screening practices for adult psychiatric patients presenting to ED — identified 8 themes (laboratory testing, history taking, vital signs, physical examination, diagnostic imaging, screening tools, ECG, system-level factors), with society recommendations discouraging routine lab testing.

  • Medical students can teach infant BLS

    Resuscitation Plus · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental study testing whether physician-supervised medical students can deliver infant BLS training to caregivers — knowledge scores improved significantly after training and were sustained at 12 months, with no difference between physician and student-led groups.

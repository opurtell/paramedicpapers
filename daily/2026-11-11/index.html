Paramedic research, 11 November 2026

7 new paramedic and prehospital papers from the 11 November 2026 scan.

Listen to the 11 November 2026 podcast episode

  • Sydney COAST registry: trauma shock first year

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Registry · Context only

    An Australian prospective multicentre registry enrolled 257 adults with prehospital trauma shock over 12 months in Sydney, finding hypotension drove 90% of shock definitions, 53% received prehospital blood, and mortality was 4.7% at 4 hours and 21% at 30 days.

  • Roadside capillary troponin for chest pain

    European Journal of Emergency Medicine · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    A French prospective multicentre diagnostic accuracy study (VALIDATE) tested prehospital capillary high-sensitivity troponin I on a point-of-care device against NSTEMI at hospital discharge in adults with acute chest pain, with treating clinicians blinded to results.

  • UK bystander CPR up, AED use flat

    Journal of Paramedic Practice · Systematic review · Context only

    A UK literature review of eight articles found bystander CPR rates increasing in England and Scotland while AED use in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest has shown little improvement, with demographics and socioeconomic factors shaping prehospital intervention rates.

  • AR-guided FAST training sped up students

    Simulation in Healthcare · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    A Japanese single-arm training study gave 18 undergraduate paramedic students a two-hour FAST course including augmented-reality-guided scans, and total examination time fell by a median 263.5 seconds with quality scores improving significantly.

  • Fibrinogen concentrate supply grows in Queensland

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Other · Context only

    A ten-year Queensland Health supply analysis of 24,717 g of fibrinogen concentrate found 97% went to 54 hospitals and only 1% to ambulance services, while population-adjusted supply rose from 8.19 to 59.2 g per 100,000 between 2016 and 2025.

  • Paramedic and midwifery students train together

    Journal of Paramedic Practice · Simulation · Context only

    A UK peer-led interprofessional simulation put paramedic and midwifery students through prehospital maternity emergencies together, with students reporting better role clarity, confidence and shared decision-making.

  • Autonomy and support, not workload, drive flourishing

    Frontiers in Psychology · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    A South African cross-sectional survey of 197 EMS personnel found emotional demands were associated with lower work capability and flourishing, while autonomy and co-worker support — not job demands — distinguished the strongest capability group.

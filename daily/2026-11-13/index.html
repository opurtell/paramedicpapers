  • GCS-based triage misses seriously injured trauma patients

    BMC Emergency Medicine · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A South Korean linked EMS–hospital cohort of 5,841 suspected severe trauma patients found weak agreement between prehospital GCS classification and diagnosis-based injury severity (κ 0.192), with one in ten patients (10.0%) severely injured despite GCS 9–15 and destination undertriage of 33.6% (GCS-based) and 47.3% (diagnosis-based), roughly doubled in rural areas.

  • Rhythm changes during CPR track neurological outcomes

    Critical Care Medicine · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    A South Korean nationwide registry study of adults with initial nonshockable OHCA found rhythm transitions during CPR graded neurological outcome: asystole converting to PEA was linked to better outcomes than persistent asystole (OR 2.28), and persistent PEA carried the best odds (OR 8.70 versus persistent asystole).

  • Night-time OHCA outcomes linked to bystander availability

    Journal of Critical Care Medicine · Systematic review · Hypothesis-generating

    A Czech-led scoping review of 18 studies found OHCA and STEMI incidence peaks in the morning with a secondary afternoon/evening peak, and night-time events were linked to lower ROSC and worse one-month and one-year survival, explained by limited bystander availability, less bystander CPR and defibrillation and longer times to hospital intervention rather than time of day itself.

  • Higher admission PaCO2 linked to worse post-arrest survival

    Journal of Critical Care Medicine · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A Chinese single-centre retrospective cohort of 617 adults resuscitated from OHCA found admission PaCO2 above 50 mmHg associated with higher one-month mortality in a stepwise gradient (each 10-mmHg increase aOR 1.45, 95% CI 1.24–1.70).

  • NSW data linkage tracks sudden cardiac arrest

    Heart Rhythm · Cohort · Context only

    An Australian data-linkage study identified 13,345 presumed sudden cardiac arrests aged 1–50 in NSW from 1.7 million linked records — 54% cardiac in aetiology and 83% fatal — and extrapolated roughly 370 cardiac SCA deaths a year among Australians aged 1–35.

  • Children's participation in ambulance care depends on clinicians

    Paramedicine · Qualitative · Context only

    A Swedish qualitative interview study of ambulance child representatives found children's participation in prehospital emergency care conceived as shaped chiefly by clinicians' attitudes and experience, then by the child's age and situational urgency, and constrained by the adult-oriented ambulance environment.

  • Systematic review of newborn PPV at birth

    Resuscitation · Systematic review · Context only

    This multinational systematic review of 42 studies (1984–2024) on positive pressure ventilation (PPV) at birth found PPV incidence of 3.0%–8.0% in population-based studies excluding CPAP, with higher rates in preterm infants, rising overall and facemask PPV over time, and stable or declining tracheal-tube PPV.

  • Paediatric prehospital fentanyl: serious adverse effects rare

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Registry · Supports current practice

    A Danish register study of 1,531 children given prehospital opioids (99.7% fentanyl) found 15.1% had at least one clinical sign compatible with opioid adverse effects, serious signs were uncommon (bradycardia and respiratory depression 0.7% each), and 63.6% were still in pain at hospital arrival.

  • CARE framework for traumatic cardiac arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · Guideline / statement · Context only

    A framework for integrating parallel priorities in traumatic cardiac arrest resuscitation — abstract not yet available, so no results to report.

  • Forecast: prehospital transfusion growth and HDFN risk

    Transfusion · Modelling · Context only

    US researchers forecast that growing prehospital transfusion of RhD-positive blood to females of childbearing potential will lead to haemolytic disease of the fetus and newborn, with one predicted case every 0.5–20 years depending on region and model.

  • First responders notifying schools after child trauma

    Journal of Child & Adolescent Trauma · Other · Context only

    A US evaluation of the Handle With Care programme linking first responders with schools after potentially traumatic child events found it feasible in an under-resourced Mississippi district, with significant gains in staff trauma-informed attitudes but low notification volume in year one.

  • Layperson CPR: pocket mask versus face shield

    Resuscitation Plus · RCT · Hypothesis-generating

    In a randomised crossover simulation study, laypersons using a pocket mask achieved the ≥200 mL mean tidal volume threshold more often than with a face shield during simulated paediatric CPR (89.2% vs 77.5%).

  • Prehospital pulse pressure flags trauma bleeding

    Injury · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A retrospective study of 2,177 adult trauma patients across three Canadian level 1 trauma centres found prehospital pulse pressure predicted active haemorrhage (AUC 0.73), with each 10 mmHg narrowing raising the odds by 51% and a cut-off of ≤45 mmHg most useful for ruling bleeding out.

  • ECPR timing in refractory OHCA: survival gap persists

    European Heart Journal. Acute Cardiovascular Care · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    A target trial emulation in 58,668 patients with refractory OHCA from the Korean nationwide registry found ECPR was associated with higher 30-day survival at 15, 30 and 60 minutes after ED arrival (+15.0, +14.4 and +7.7 percentage points); an exploratory analysis found the benefit narrowed but remained significant to 120 minutes.

  • Instrumented manikin neck for immobilisation training

    Proceedings of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers. Part H, Journal of Engineering in Medicine · Simulation · Context only

    Australian engineers built and tested a proof-of-concept instrumented manikin neck with six-degree-of-freedom motion and real-time head-position feedback for immobilisation training, achieving angle measurement error under 0.89° versus motion capture.

  • Racial gaps in bystander CPR for opioid OHCA

    Resuscitation · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective cohort study using NEMSIS data (n=19,612) found Black patients with opioid-associated OHCA had significantly lower odds of receiving bystander CPR than White patients (AOR 0.70, 95% CI 0.63-0.77), with Hispanic patients also less likely (AOR 0.82).

  • RhD-positive prehospital blood OK for women

    Prehospital Emergency Care · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Narrative review synthesising evidence on D-alloimmunization and HDFN risk found modeling studies estimate fetal death due to anti-D-mediated HDFN would occur in fewer than 0.1% of females of childbearing potential exposed to RhD-positive blood products in trauma, supporting prehospital RhD-positive transfusion when RhD-negative unavailable.

Daily scan

Paramedic research, 18 November 2026

A quiet day. The standout is a Turkish study of 2.3 million emergency calls finding a centralised call centre linked to markedly leaner ambulance operations, alongside Finnish–New Zealand data showing 70% of fatal brain bleeds never reach a neurosurgical unit.

  • Centralised call centres linked to leaner ambulance operations

    Pakistan Journal of Medical Sciences · Cohort · Context only

    A retrospective before–after study of 2,346,774 emergency calls in Ordu province, Türkiye found a centralised emergency call centre was associated with a 49% drop in calls per incident and a 36.9% fall in ambulance response time.

  • Most fatal brain bleeds die before neurosurgery

    JAMA Network Open · Cohort · Context only

    A Finnish–New Zealand population cohort of 12,912 patients with aneurysmal subarachnoid haemorrhage found 70.5% of 30-day deaths occurred without neurosurgical unit admission, with higher proportions in districts farther from a neurosurgical centre.

  • Breathlessness self-management linked to fewer ambulance calls

    BMC Pulmonary Medicine · Other · Context only

    An Australian co-designed feasibility study of a home-based breathlessness intervention service found 140 completers over 22 months, with 90% reporting improvement in daily activities and 21% reporting they had avoided calling an ambulance for breathlessness.

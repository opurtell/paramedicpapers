  • Eye emergency calls often chemical or stroke

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Registry · Context only

    Register-based cohort of 340 eye-related emergency calls found chemical exposures (31.5%), vision loss (25.9%), and trauma (18.2%) as leading causes, with 11.5% of patients initially coded for eye problems later diagnosed with stroke.

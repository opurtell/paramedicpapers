  • No risky oxygen build-up at defibrillation site in CPR

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Mannequin study testing whether removing oxygen-delivery devices reduces ambient oxygen concentration at defibrillation sites — it found no clinically relevant oxygen accumulation, and removing devices made no difference to oxygen levels.

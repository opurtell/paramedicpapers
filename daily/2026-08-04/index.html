Paramedic research, 4 August 2026

8 new paramedic and prehospital papers from the 4 August 2026 scan.

  • Cold-water immersion in heatstroke cardiac arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · Other · Context only

    Examines feasibility and outcomes of cold-water immersion during prehospital heatstroke cardiac arrest resuscitation

  • High adverse event rate after prehospital RSI

    Prehospital Emergency Care · Cross-sectional · Supports current practice

    Investigates the prevalence of adverse events following prehospital airway management using neuromuscular blockade

  • NEMSIS records mostly unique encounters

    Prehospital Emergency Care · Cross-sectional · Context only

    Retrospective analysis of 36.7 million NEMSIS records — 99.5% represent unique patient-care episodes with only 0.5% involving multiple records for the same encounter

  • FDSV rarely detected in virtual ED

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective cohort of Victoria's Virtual ED — only 21 FDSV presentations identified among 173,936 visits (0.012%), suggesting significant under-detection

  • Single-dose aminoglycoside AKI risk in CKD

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective cohort across four Australian hospitals — single-dose aminoglycosides caused AKI in 30.9% of CKD patients vs 13.4% without CKD (OR 2.88)

  • AI triage tool predicts ED admission

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Other · Context only

    Model explainability analysis of START-AI — AUROC improved from 0.78 to 0.90 with AI features, driven primarily by blood test results, triage comments, and clinical notes

  • Vaccine-preventable diseases re-emerging in EDs

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Editorial on the re-emergence of vaccine-preventable diseases post-COVID — declining vaccination rates are causing outbreaks of diphtheria, measles, and other VPDs in Australasia

  • EDs: opportunistic immunisation, not vaccine clinics

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Editorial arguing EDs should provide opportunistic, proportionate immunisation for vulnerable patients rather than functioning as routine vaccine clinics

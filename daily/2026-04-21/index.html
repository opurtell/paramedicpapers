Paramedic research, 21 April 2026

3 new paramedic and prehospital papers from the 21 April 2026 scan.

  • Paramedic patella reduction guideline works in Victoria

    Prehospital Emergency Care · Cohort · Supports current practice

    Retrospective study (n=1,210) evaluating whether Ambulance Victoria's Nov 2022 CPG for paramedic patella reduction changed practice — 91.8% reduction success rate, transport rate dropped 38.4%, case duration cut by ~33 minutes, and IV cannula use down 17.3%, with no change in effective analgesia rates.

  • Pacing artefact can mask VF during TCP

    Resuscitation · Case series · Context only

    Case series examining the problem of ventricular arrhythmias occurring during TCP and delays to defibrillation — highlights the clinical trap of pacing artefact masking VF and the need to pause pacing to identify shockable rhythms.

  • Bystander care in first minutes of trauma

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Concept editorial arguing that the first minutes after injury, before EMS arrival, are a distinct high-risk window where bystander action and dispatcher guidance can prevent deaths, especially through haemorrhage control and airway management.

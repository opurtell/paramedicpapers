Paramedic research, 26 May 2026

2 new paramedic and prehospital papers from the 26 May 2026 scan.

  • ChatGPT-4o generates neonatal resus scenarios

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    This prospective multicenter pilot tested whether ChatGPT-4o and DeepSeek-R1 could generate valid neonatal resuscitation simulation scenarios for training, finding ChatGPT-4o performed comparably to official NRP scenarios in overall quality while demonstrating strengths in debriefing design but having gaps in dynamic vital sign provision.

  • Tatra mountain rescue epidemiology over 10 years

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Registry · Context only

    Retrospective analysis of 9,477 mountain rescue missions over 10 years found a median NACA score of 2 with 38% classified as moderate-to-severe, seasonal variation in severity, and helicopter use correlated with higher acuity, providing epidemiological data that could inform training and resource planning for wilderness EMS.

Support

Support Paramedic Papers

Paramedic Papers is free, has no ads and always will. If the daily papers, podcast or PD brief help your practice, you can chip in towards the running costs.

Contribute on Ko-fi

Any amount helps, and a one-off contribution is all that's needed. You can pay by card or PayPal, and you don't need a Ko-fi account.

What it pays for

  • The server that finds, screens and summarises new papers every morning.
  • The AI and text-to-speech services behind the summaries and the daily podcast.
  • Audio hosting, email sending for the PD brief, and the domain.

Good to know

  • Everything on the site stays free whether or not you contribute. There are no supporter-only features.
  • Contributions don't buy influence. Papers are picked and summarised the same way for everyone, and nothing is paid placement.
  • Paramedic Papers isn't a registered charity, so contributions aren't tax deductible.
  • Payments are handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers (Stripe or PayPal). This site never sees your card details. See the privacy page for what's shared.

Other ways to help

  • Tell a colleague, or share an episode or paper with your station.
  • Sign up for the PD brief.
  • Spotted an error? Email newsletter@paramedicpapers.com.

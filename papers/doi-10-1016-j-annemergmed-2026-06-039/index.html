  • Low analgesia rates in sickle cell EMS pain

    The American Journal of Emergency Medicine · 18 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Context only

    US nationwide cross-section of 10,023 9-1-1 ALS encounters for sickle cell disease complications in 2023 — pain scores documented in 73% but only 13.5% received any analgesia (11.7% opioids), and among patients scoring ≥7/10, contact times ≥20 min were associated with roughly five-fold more IV access and ten-fold more analgesia/opioid administration.

  • IV magnesium adds little for ED headache

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 25 Aug 2026 · RCT · Context only

    RCT of 1,028 ED patients with acute nontraumatic headache found IV magnesium sulfate plus paracetamol achieved more frequent treatment success (78.9% vs 65.1%) but pain score differences were below clinical importance thresholds, with less rescue analgesia needed but more mild side effects.

  • Intranasal equals subcutaneous ketamine for pain

    Annals of Emergency Medicine · 20 Aug 2026 · RCT · Supports current practice

    RCT testing whether intranasal ketamine is non-inferior to subcutaneous for acute traumatic pain — both routes provided equivalent analgesia with minimal clinically important differences, though minor adverse events were more common with subcutaneous administration.

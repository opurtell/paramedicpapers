Mapping medical clearance of psychiatric ED patients

Medical Screening of Adult Psychiatric Patients Presenting to the Emergency Department
Annals of Emergency Medicine · Added 20 August 2026

Scoping review mapping medical screening practices for adult psychiatric patients presenting to ED — identified 8 themes (laboratory testing, history taking, vital signs, physical examination, diagnostic imaging, screening tools, ECG, system-level factors), with society recommendations discouraging routine lab testing.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Scoping review with thematic analysis
Participants
145 publications; 62 original studies covering 34,836 patients and 1,149 clinicians
Main finding
Eight screening themes identified; laboratory testing most studied (62.9% of original studies); most society recommendations discourage routine laboratory testing
Caveat
Predominantly retrospective original studies; no assessment of diagnostic accuracy
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Prehospital clinicians frequently manage psychiatric presentations; findings on unnecessary testing patterns are relevant but ED-focused.

Learning goal

Weigh the value of routine laboratory screening when assessing adult psychiatric patients before ED handover.

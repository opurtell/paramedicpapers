AI predicts ED admitting team from triage notes

Using Sydney Triage to Admission Risk Tool With Artificial Intelligence (START-AI) to Predict Inpatient Admitting Specialty for Emergency Department Patients: Single Centre Deep Learning Analysis
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 8 September 2026

Sydney single-centre deep learning study of 40,054 admitted ED patients — a fine-tuned clinical language model predicted the inpatient admitting specialty from triage notes with AUROC 0.97, though precision was a moderate 0.71.

Study type
Modelling
Design
Single-centre deep learning analysis of electronic medical record data
Participants
40,054 admitted adult ED patients in Sydney
Main finding
Bio-ClinicalBERT predicted one of 20 inpatient admitting teams with weighted AUROC 0.97 (95% CI 0.96-0.98), precision 0.71, accuracy 0.75 (0.88 when top two teams accepted)
Caveat
Single centre; moderate precision and only ~75% single-label accuracy; needs external validation

Learning goal

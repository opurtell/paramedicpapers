A framework for caring at the scene: STOP

Acute Suffering, Act of Togetherness and On-Scene Space: A Concept Synthesis for Caring in Prehospital Emergency Care
Scandinavian Journal of Caring Sciences · 2026-12 · Added 12 November 2026

A Swedish-Finnish concept synthesis developed the STOP framework — acute Suffering, act of Togetherness, On-scene space — positioning holistic, patient-centred caring, not just biomedical intervention, as the core of prehospital emergency care.

Overdrive pacing for refractory pVT prehospital

Transcutaneous overdrive pacing for refractory polymorphic ventricular tachycardia in the prehospital setting: A case report and practical considerations
Emergency Medical Service · Added 27 October 2026

A prehospital case report describes transcutaneous overdrive pacing used as rescue therapy for refractory polymorphic ventricular tachycardia after cardiac arrest, highlighting the gap between guideline options and what ambulances actually carry.

Bottom line

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Transcutaneous pacing is an intensive care paramedic skill on Australian trucks, and this is a practical rescue option when drugs and shocks fail — but it rests on a single case.

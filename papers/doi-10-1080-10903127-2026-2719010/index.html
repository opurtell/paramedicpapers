Big shift to online EMS continuing education

Trends in CAPCE-accredited Emergency Medical Services Continuing Education Over the Past Decade
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 25 August 2026

Retrospective analysis of 19.3 million CE completions showed post-pandemic shift to online formats, with asynchronous distributive learning increasing 209% and interactive gaming courses increasing 915%.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective interrupted time-series analysis of CAPCE-accredited continuing education completions
Participants
19,289,785 EMS CE completions over 120 months (July 2014–June 2024)
Main finding
Post-pandemic, asynchronous distributive learning completions rose 209.4% (86,242 to 266,842/month), interactive gaming courses rose 914.6% (98 to 992), virtual instructor-led training rose 218.9% (2,096 to 8,151), while live in-person courses fell 8.1%
Caveat
US CAPCE database; association with NREMT policy change, not causal
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). transferability — US-focused but reflects broader education trends — online CE formats are becoming dominant globally

Learning goal

Understand how online and simulation-based formats shape the quality of my ongoing paramedic education.

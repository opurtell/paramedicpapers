Electric ambulances feasible in most dispatches

Feasibility of Electric Vehicle Adoption in US Ambulance Fleets: A Retrospective Analysis of NEMSIS Data
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 28 May 2026

Retrospective NEMSIS analysis examined operational feasibility of electric ambulance adoption — fleet integration appears viable under specific conditions, though infrastructure readiness remains key.

Study type
Modelling
Design
Retrospective analysis of NEMSIS 2024 data with modelling of EV ambulance ranges
Participants
US 9-1-1 EMS calls from NEMSIS 2024 (number not stated)
Main finding
Estimated mileage fell within EV range requirements for 97.6-99.9% of dispatches depending on urbanicity and vehicle capacity; at-hospital Level-2 and Level-3 charging restored significant fractions of energy used.
Caveat
Modelled hypothetical vehicle profiles rather than real-world EV ambulance operations
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how electric ambulance adoption could shape fleet operations and readiness in the Australian context.

AU/NZ emergency care research priorities being set

Establishing the Priorities for Emergency Care Research in Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand
Emergency Medicine Australasia · 2026-10 · Added 23 October 2026

ACEM's Clinical Trials Network, with the James Lind Alliance, has launched a priority setting partnership to set the emergency care research agenda for Australia and Aotearoa New Zealand for the next 5–10 years.

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). It's an ED-led process rather than an ambulance one, but the resulting agenda shapes the emergency care evidence Australian paramedics work from, and the partnership is explicitly seeking clinician and consumer participation.

Bystander support pathway after OHCA feasibility pilot

RescQ™: Study protocol for a feasibility pilot of structured support for bystanders/lay-rescuers following out-of-hospital cardiac arrest
Resuscitation Plus · Added 25 August 2026

Feasibility pilot protocol tested structured support pathway for bystanders involved in OHCA, including digital resources and psychological screening via liaison officers.

Study type
Other
Design
12-month non-randomised prospective feasibility pilot protocol of a structured bystander support pathway
Participants
Not reported (protocol; adult bystanders of OHCA in Essex, UK)
Main finding
Describes pathway of on-scene signposting, app registration, web-based resources, and liaison officer contact with psychological screening; primary outcome is feasibility; pilot completed with analysis underway
Caveat
Protocol only — no results yet
Bottom line
Context only

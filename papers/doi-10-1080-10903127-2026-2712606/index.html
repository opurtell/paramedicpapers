Rural vs urban EMS: 2024 NEMSIS update

National Characteristics of Emergency Medical Services in Rural and Frontier Areas: A 2024 Update on Rural-Urban Differences in EMS Care
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 31 July 2026

Survey examining EMS characteristics and care differences between rural and urban areas — reports on resource availability, response times, and clinical capabilities.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Cross-sectional analysis of a national EMS registry (NEMSIS 2024)
Participants
45,383,694 EMS responses across 50 US states and DC; 6.9% rural
Main finding
Rural responses showed older patients, greater air-medical use and higher on-scene mortality; the rural trauma on-scene death excess was largely explained by patient physiology at first EMS contact; the 2012 rural stroke-mortality gap was absent in 2024
Caveat
US registry data; limited to recorded NEMSIS variables
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how rural context shapes prehospital patient physiology, resource use and outcomes in Australian settings.

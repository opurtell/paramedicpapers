Virtual triage model feasible for ambulance patients

Feasibility of a Pre-Hospital Virtual Triage and Care Model: A Mixed-Methods Pilot in Emergency Care
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 23 April 2026

They tested a pre-hospital telehealth model for ambulance-retrieved patients, and found it was feasible, advanced senior review, and reduced ED length of stay without improving crew cycle times.

Study type
Other
Design
Mixed-methods pilot with matched cohort analysis
Participants
26 eligible patients; piloted in 10 cases over 3 days
Main finding
Senior medical assessment 81 minutes earlier, ED length of stay reduced 14% (50 minutes); no improvement in crew cycle times; high patient satisfaction
Caveat
Very small pilot sample (10 cases) over 3 days
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

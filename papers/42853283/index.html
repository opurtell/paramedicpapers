Italian HEMS trauma mortality predictors

Prehospital severity and in-hospital mortality in trauma patients managed by the Friuli-Venezia Giulia helicopter emergency medical service: a 6-year retrospective cohort study
European Journal of Trauma and Emergency Surgery · 2026-10-09 · Added 11 October 2026

Six-year Italian HEMS cohort of 3,152 transported trauma patients found 3.4% in-hospital mortality, independently predicted by older age, lower Revised Trauma Score, intubation and prehospital CPR — the last two acting mainly as severity markers.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective observational cohort
Participants
3,143 HEMS-managed trauma patients with outcome data
Main finding
In-hospital mortality 3.4%; independently associated with RTS <4 (OR 5.19, 95% CI 1.64–17.04), RTS 4–<7 (OR 2.36), older age (OR 1.03 per year), tracheal intubation and prehospital CPR
Caveat
Single-region HEMS cohort with internal-only model validation and residual severity confounding around intubation and CPR
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Low relevance). Physician-staffed HEMS model with dispatch, crewing and transport thresholds quite different from Australian road and rotary-wing services.

Learning goal

Freeze-dried plasma complements whole blood

The role of freeze-dried plasma in a world of whole blood: a Canadian prehospital and transport perspective
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 14 May 2026

This commentary argues freeze-dried plasma complements whole blood in prehospital resuscitation, offering a scalable, shelf-stable solution for rural and remote regions with long transport times.

Nursing triage score for prehospital trauma in China

Development and validation of a nursing-oriented triage decision tool for prehospital trauma patients in China: a retrospective cohort study
BMC Emergency Medicine · 2026-09-05 · Added 28 November 2026

A Chinese retrospective cohort of 2,864 prehospital trauma patients developed and internally validated a five-variable nursing triage score (GCS, SBP < 90 mmHg, active haemorrhage, fatal trauma type, age ≥ 65) for 24-hour mortality, ISS ≥ 16 or emergent surgery, with AUROC 0.883 in the validation set.

Caveat

Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). China's nurse-led prehospital crews are a different model from Australian paramedic triage, and the score was only internally validated, so it is background for trauma triage tools rather than something to adopt.

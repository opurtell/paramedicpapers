Medical planning for 2024 Republican convention

Health and Medical Preparedness and Response to the 2024 Republican National Convention
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 15 May 2026

This case report describes comprehensive health and medical planning and multi-agency response for a large-scale national political convention, providing a template for mass gathering EMS preparedness.

Study type
Case series
Design
Observational case report of EMS records and event planning documents
Participants
Not reported (event attended by ~50,000 people)
Main finding
No mass casualty incidents, mass arrests, public health emergencies or staffing crises; low patient presentation rates and on-scene prehospital care minimised impact on local health systems.
Caveat
Single US mass-gathering event; descriptive
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how multi-agency planning shapes prehospital care at large-scale mass gathering events.

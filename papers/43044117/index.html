Protocol: climate and emergency dispatch volumes

Impact of Climatic and Environmental Conditions on Prehospital Emergency Medical Dispatches in Germany: Protocol for an Exploratory, Population-Based Observational Study
JMIR Research Protocols · 2026-11-17 · Added 19 November 2026

A German study protocol plans to link emergency medical dispatch data from two northern metropolitan regions with high-resolution climate data to model temperature–dispatch relationships — no results yet.

Main finding
Protocol only — will assess nonlinear temperature-response relationships, temporal lags and spatial variation in dispatch volumes across Hanover and Brunswick, comparing high-resolution versus station-based exposure data.
Caveat
A protocol with no findings; value depends on data access that isn't yet proven.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Heat already drives Australian dispatch surges and this protocol signals a rigorous European entry into the same question Australian services face now, using dispatch data rather than hospital outcomes.

Learning goal

Understand how temperature extremes and environmental conditions shape demand patterns across Australian ambulance dispatch systems.

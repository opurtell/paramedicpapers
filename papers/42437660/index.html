Miller vs Macintosh blade in paediatric intubation

Initial Laryngoscope Blade Selection and Outcomes in Paediatric Emergency Department Intubations: Miller vs. Macintosh
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 27 July 2026

In this cohort of young children intubated in the ED, Macintosh blade use was associated with higher FPS and fewer hypoxic events compared with Miller blade.

Study type
Registry
Design
Prospectively collected registry cohort (ANZEDAR)
Participants
201 children under 5 intubated in ED
Main finding
Macintosh blades had higher FPS (80.5% vs 63.6%; OR 0.42, 95% CI 0.22–0.80) and lower hypoxia (17.7% vs 33.0%; OR 2.28, 95% CI 1.19–4.46) than Miller; hypotension rates did not differ
Caveat
Miller-blade children were substantially younger, making age a significant confounder; in-hospital ED setting
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the choice of curved versus straight laryngoscope blades when intubating young children, considering age-related factors.

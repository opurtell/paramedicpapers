Methoxyflurane feasibility confirmed in Canada

Prehospital analgesia in adults using inhaled methoxyflurane: a feasibility study
Pain Management · 2026-10-01 · Added 3 October 2026

Canadian feasibility study of patient-controlled inhaled methoxyflurane in 97 adult trauma patients found 95% protocol compliance, median pain falling from 8/10 to 5/10, mostly mild side effects in 23%, and no serious drug-related adverse events.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Single-centre prospective observational feasibility study
Participants
97 adult trauma patients (92 treated)
Main finding
Protocol compliance 95%; median NRS pain 8/10 pre-treatment to 5/10 post-treatment; side effects in 23%, mostly mild, with no serious methoxyflurane-related adverse events
Caveat
Feasibility study with no comparator arm, so the pain improvement can't be attributed to the drug alone
Bottom line
Supports current practice

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Methoxyflurane is carried across Australian services as standard, so independent real-world safety and feasibility data feed straight back into how it's used here.

Learning goal

Strengthen my judgement on when patient-controlled inhaled methoxyflurane is appropriate for adult trauma pain.

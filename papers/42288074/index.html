Airway pressures during manual vs mechanical CPR

Mean airway pressure as the missing link in CPR physiology: A prehospital comparison of manual and mechanical chest compressions
The American Journal of Emergency Medicine · Added 27 July 2026

Prehospital mPAW values were substantially lower than those reported in previous ED-based studies.

Study type
Other
Design
Prospective exploratory pilot observational study
Participants
19 adults with OHCA (10 manual, 9 mechanical compressions)
Main finding
Median initial mean airway pressure was 7.74 mbar; higher early with mechanical (8.96 vs 6.54 mbar); ROSC patients had higher mPAW but not statistically significant
Caveat
Very small sample, single Belgian service, exploratory
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Strengthen my understanding of how airway pressures during chest compressions influence ventilation and perfusion in cardiac arrest.

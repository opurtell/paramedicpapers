Swedish public CPR willingness and AED awareness

Willingness to act in a cardiac arrest and awareness of automated external defibrillators: a survey of the Swedish population
Resuscitation Plus · Added 7 August 2026

Population-based survey found 80% of Swedish adults willing to perform CPR on a stranger, with recent training increasing confidence and awareness, but only 20% know where their nearest AED is located at home.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Population-based cross-sectional survey of a Swedish online panel
Participants
1026 adults aged 18–84 years
Main finding
80% would act if a stranger had a cardiac arrest; ~half felt confident using an AED; 50% knew the nearest AED at work but only ~20% at home; recent CPR training was associated with greater willingness to act
Caveat
Survey of a single country's population; self-reported willingness may not reflect actual behaviour
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Relevant to understanding bystander CPR willingness and AED awareness barriers, but Swedish-specific data may not directly translate to Australian community context.

Learning goal

Understand the barriers that stop bystanders starting CPR and using public AEDs in Australian communities.

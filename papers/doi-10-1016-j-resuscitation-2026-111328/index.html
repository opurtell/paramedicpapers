2026 International Liaison Committee on Resuscitation Consensus on Science With Treatment Recommendations: Summary from the Basic Life Support; Advanced Life Support; Pediatric Life Support; Neonatal Life Support; Education, Implementation, and Teams; and First Aid Task Forces
Resuscitation · 2026-11 · Added 6 November 2026

This annual ILCOR Consensus on Science with Treatment Recommendations (multinational) systematically reviewed the latest resuscitation evidence across six task forces (BLS, ALS, paediatric, neonatal, education/implementation/teams, and first aid) using GRADE criteria; no single headline finding or figure is reported.

  • Long-term life after ECPR for cardiac arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · 6 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    An Italian tertiary centre followed refractory OHCA survivors treated with ECPR (63 of 406 discharged alive) and their caregivers, finding long-term survival largely confined to good neurological recovery but preserved quality of life, with cognitive impairment and sleep problems common and anxiety more frequent in caregivers.

  • Post-ROSC ST elevation tracks acute coronary occlusion

    Resuscitation · 4 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A Japanese single-centre review of 149 angiography-evaluable OHCA survivors with presumed culprit lesions found post-resuscitation ST-segment elevation marked an acute occlusive coronary phenotype but was not independently linked to neurological outcome once prehospital factors were accounted for.

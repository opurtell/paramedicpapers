Mapping chest pain risk tools in ambulance care

Chest pain risk-stratification tools used or trialled in Ambulance Health Service settings: A Scoping Review
Paramedicine · 2026-11-07 · Added 8 November 2026

A scoping review of 27 articles mapped eight chest-pain risk-stratification tools used or trialled in ambulance settings, finding the HEART score and derivatives most studied but with varying and incomplete validation for ambulance use.

Eight chest-pain risk-stratification tools were used or trialled in ambulance settings; HEART and its derivatives were the most frequently studied, with performance varying across settings and validation for ambulance use incomplete
Caveat

Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Safely triaging chest pain without transport is an active question for Australian ambulance services, but the tools reviewed are mostly hospital-derived and inconsistently validated for prehospital use.

Learning goal

Compare chest pain risk-stratification tools and their validity for prehospital use to strengthen my cardiac triage decisions.

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 5 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A US EMS agency reviewed continuous monitor ECGs from 56 patients given prehospital transcutaneous pacing between 2017 and 2024 and found electrical capture in only 32%, often intermittent, with clinician-verbalised pulses coinciding with capture in just 15% of instances.

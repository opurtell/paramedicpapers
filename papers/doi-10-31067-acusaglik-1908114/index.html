Field acuity predicts ICU admission in stable adults

Field Acuity, Triage Concordance, and Clinical Outcomes in Communicative and Clinically Stable Adults Transported by 112 Ambulance Services
Acıbadem Üniversitesi Sağlık Bilimleri Dergisi · Added 18 May 2026

Prospective study of 393 patients transported by ambulance found urgent field classification, hypotension, critical hyperglycemia, and prehospital interventions predicted ICU admission.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Prospective single-centre observational study
Participants
393 communicative, clinically stable adults transported by ambulance to a tertiary ED
Main finding
ICU admission 15.8%, 30-day mortality 5.4%. Agreement between field and ED urgency was weak (κ=0.06). Urgent field classification, critical hyperglycemia, hypotension and any prehospital intervention independently predicted ICU admission.
Caveat
Single Turkish centre; excluded non-communicative or unstable patients, limiting generalisability.
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how field acuity classification and prehospital physiological findings relate to later deterioration in adults who appear clinically stable.

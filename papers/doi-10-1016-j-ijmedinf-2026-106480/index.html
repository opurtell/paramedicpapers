AI in prehospital airway care still experimental

From decision support to clinical integration: A scoping review of artificial intelligence in prehospital airway management
International Journal of Medical Informatics · Added 23 May 2026

They reviewed AI applications in prehospital airway management and found most tools remain experimental, with limited integration into clinical workflows despite promising capabilities for difficult airway prediction and real-time guidance.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR)
Participants
9 studies published 2020–2026
Main finding
AI applications spanned prediction/triage, physiological monitoring, documentation NLP, and computer vision for intubation; reported AUCs 0.867–0.960, but all studies used retrospective data with internal validation and none were prospective trials.
Caveat
Only nine small studies, all retrospective with limited external validation
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the readiness of AI decision support for difficult airway prediction in my prehospital practice.

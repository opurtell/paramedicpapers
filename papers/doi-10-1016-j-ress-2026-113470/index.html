Drone AED network modelling for Scotland

A Bayesian learning approach for drone coverage network: A case study on cardiac arrest in Scotland
Reliability Engineering & System Safety · Added 25 September 2026

Bayesian optimisation of a drone-assisted AED delivery network using geographically referenced Scottish cardiac arrest data — station placement driven by OHCA survival probability and existing EMS coverage, with cost-effectiveness analysis suggesting drone AED delivery is cost-effective and could meaningfully improve response coverage in rural areas with long ambulance response times.

  • Higher CPR SpO2 linked to ROSC, not good outcome

    Resuscitation · 25 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Study of 98 OHCAs asking whether pulse oximetry readings during CPR relate to outcomes — higher event-average SpO2 was associated with sustained ROSC (aOR 1.05, AUC 0.77, cutpoint ~71%) and survival to admission, but not neurological intact survival, and cycle-average SpO2 did not track compression quality metrics.

  • rCAST score in young children after arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · 25 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    External validation of the rCAST post-cardiac arrest prognostic score in 75 infants and young children after targeted temperature management — good discrimination (AUC 0.947 for unfavourable neurologic outcome, 0.857 for mortality) with no favourable outcomes in the high-severity group, but 76% of low-score patients did well and the authors caution against using it for clinical decisions.

  • Dispatcher CPR barriers delay bystander CPR, cut survival

    Resuscitation · 24 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective cohort of 3,263 transcribed Swedish 112 calls linked to the national cardiac arrest registry quantified barriers to dispatcher-assisted CPR after the dispatcher recognised arrest — 43.7% of calls hit at least one barrier (commonest: inability to move the patient), bystander CPR reached 85% in barrier-free calls but stayed below 50% with three or more, and any barrier was associated with lower ROSC (adjusted RR 0.89) and 30-day survival (adjusted RR 0.57).

  • Night drone AED delivery feasible but workload high

    Resuscitation Plus · 24 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental mixed-methods study of 24 human-supervised drone flights delivering a 480 g CellAED over 877 m in a semi-urban area — all flights completed without safety incidents and night flight times matched day (mean ~140 s vs ~136 s, p=0.43), though pilots reported markedly higher cognitive workload and environmental complexity after dark.

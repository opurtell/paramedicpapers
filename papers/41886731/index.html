Paramedic patella reduction guideline works in Victoria

Paramedic Patella Reduction — Ambulance Victoria CPG Evaluation
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 21 April 2026

Retrospective study (n=1,210) evaluating whether Ambulance Victoria's Nov 2022 CPG for paramedic patella reduction changed practice — 91.8% reduction success rate, transport rate dropped 38.4%, case duration cut by ~33 minutes, and IV cannula use down 17.3%, with no change in effective analgesia rates.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective interrupted time series using Ambulance Victoria electronic patient care data
Participants
1,210 patients with acute patella dislocation (622 pre-, 588 post-guideline)
Main finding
No significant step change in effective analgesia (0.8%, 95%CI -6.6% to 8.1%; p=0.832); significant reductions in IV access (-17.3%), transport rate (-38.4%) and case duration (~33 minutes shorter)
Caveat
Retrospective design; analgesia unchanged, and longer-term functional outcomes and safety not assessed
Bottom line
Supports current practice

Learning goal

Strengthen my judgement on when prehospital patella reduction can avoid transport while maintaining effective analgesia.

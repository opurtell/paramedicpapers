Roadside capillary troponin for chest pain

Diagnostic accuracy of prehospital capillary high-sensitivity cardiac troponin I in patients with acute chest pain: the prospective multicenter VALIDATE study
European Journal of Emergency Medicine · 2026-11-10 · Added 11 November 2026

A French prospective multicentre diagnostic accuracy study (VALIDATE) tested prehospital capillary high-sensitivity troponin I on a point-of-care device against NSTEMI at hospital discharge in adults with acute chest pain, with treating clinicians blinded to results.

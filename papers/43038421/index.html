Prehospital STEMI thrombolysis is value for money

Cost-effectiveness of paramedic-administered prehospital thrombolysis for ST elevation myocardial infarction
Prehospital Emergency Care · 2026-11-16 · Added 19 November 2026

An Australian microsimulation of the Queensland prehospital thrombolysis program estimated paramedic-administered STEMI thrombolysis costs AUD 16,750 per QALY gained, concluding it is value for money.

Decision-analytic microsimulation model (health-provider perspective, lifetime horizon)

Modelled cohort from all QAS STEMI patients receiving prehospital thrombolysis, 2022–2024
Main finding
AUD 16,750 per QALY gained (95% uncertainty range 15,477–18,423), equivalent to USD 11,725; results robust in sensitivity analysis.
Caveat
A model with a hypothetical counterfactual comparator rather than a randomised comparison, and long-term inputs drawn from published literature.
Bottom line
Supports current practice

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Australian research on a program paramedics deliver; the cost-per-QALY framing is directly useful for service planning and funding arguments anywhere long PCI transfer times exist.

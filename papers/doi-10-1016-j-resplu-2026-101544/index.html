Machine learning predicts good recovery after OHCA

Early Prediction of Good Neurological Recovery in Comatose Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest Survivors Using Machine Learning: A Multicenter Prospective Registry Study
Resuscitation Plus · 2026-10 · Added 30 October 2026

A South Korean multicentre prospective registry study developed a machine-learning model for comatose OHCA survivors that predicted good 6-month neurological recovery before targeted temperature management with AUROC 0.929, outperforming conventional scores like CAHP.

Study type
Registry
Design
Multicentre prospective registry (Korean Hypothermia Network, KORHN-PRO)
Participants
1,358 comatose adult OHCA survivors treated with targeted temperature management
Main finding
The voting-classifier model predicted 6-month neurologically intact survival (29.3% of patients) with AUROC 0.929 and AUPRC 0.850; the top five predictors were prehospital initial rhythm, arrival rhythm, age, PaCO2 and total collapse duration
Caveat
Single-country registry from 2015–2018 and the model has not been externally validated
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Prognostication happens in intensive care, but the strongest predictors are the prehospital variables crews generate — initial rhythm, collapse duration — so early, accurate handover documentation is where this touches the road.

Opioid OHCA outcomes similar to other arrests

Opioid-Associated vs Undifferentiated Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: Examining differences in outcomes and the relationship with Utstein variables
Resuscitation · 2026-10 · Added 11 October 2026

Analysis of 10,786 EMS-treated OHCAs in a Canadian registry found opioid-associated arrests had similar outcomes to undifferentiated arrests (OR 1.02), but Utstein variables like initial rhythm and witnessed status predicted outcome differently in the opioid group.

Study type
Registry
Design
Retrospective registry cohort with toxicological case classification
Participants
10,786 EMS-treated OHCA (1,349 opioid-associated)
Main finding
No association between opioid-associated OHCA and favourable neurological outcome (OR 1.02, 95% CI 0.77–1.35), but significant interactions for sex (p=0.032), initial rhythm (p=0.024) and bystander-witnessed status (p<0.001)
Caveat
Opioid classification relied on post-mortem toxicology and death certificates, so misclassification is likely
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Opioid-toxicity arrests are a growing part of the Australian OHCA caseload and this is a large EMS-treated registry rather than an in-hospital cohort.

Learning goal

Understand how patient context in opioid-associated cardiac arrest shapes the prognostic value of Utstein variables.

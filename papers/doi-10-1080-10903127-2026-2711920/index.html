NEMSIS records mostly unique encounters

The estimated prevalence of multiple record encounters in the National Emergency Medical Services Information System
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 4 August 2026

Retrospective analysis of 36.7 million NEMSIS records — 99.5% represent unique patient-care episodes with only 0.5% involving multiple records for the same encounter

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Retrospective observational analysis of NEMSIS 2023 dataset using record-clustering algorithm
Participants
36,757,562 EMS records
Main finding
99.5% of episodes of care consisted of a single EMS record; only ~0.5% involved multiple records for the same encounter
Caveat
Algorithm-based identification of related records; potential misclassification of clustered encounters
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). transferability — Australian paramedics don't use NEMSIS, but data quality issues are universal for anyone doing EMS research or evaluating large datasets

Learning goal

Understand how linked EMS records for a single episode of care affect documentation and continuity across Australian systems.

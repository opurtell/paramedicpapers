What running an ambulance trial really takes

Integrating clinical research into emergency medical services: lessons from the PreMeFen trial
Trials · 2026-08-19 · Added 17 November 2026

A Norwegian survey of 67 ambulance personnel in the PreMeFen RCT found integrating research into EMS was feasible but demanding: 28% protocol deviations across 338 inclusions, motivation declining over 18 months, and 51% finding study communication excessive — yet 90% would take part in future research.

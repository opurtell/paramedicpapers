Only a third get PHT within 30 min

Determinants of Timely Prehospital Thrombolysis Administration within a Pharmacoinvasive STEMI Network in Victoria, Australia
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 30 July 2026

This Australian study identifies factors affecting timely prehospital thrombolysis delivery in Victoria's pharmacoinvasive STEMI network.

Study type
Registry
Design
Retrospective registry cohort, Victorian Ambulance STEMI Quality Initiative
Participants
1440 adults treated with paramedic thrombolysis for suspected STEMI, 2018-2023
Main finding
32.5% received prehospital thrombolysis within 30 min of first medical contact (median 37 min); delay associated with mild pain severity (aOR 2.08), clinical consultation and after-hours presentation, while ECG within 10 min reduced delay (aOR 0.40); times improved 17% per year (aOR 0.83)
Caveat
Retrospective registry data; suspected rather than confirmed STEMI
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Strengthen my judgement on delivering timely prehospital thrombolysis for STEMI within national pharmacoinvasive pathways.

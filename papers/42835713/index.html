EMS dashboards: thin crisis evidence

Performance Indicators in Emergency Medical Services: A Scoping Review of Dashboard Applications in the Context of Crisis Management
Archives of Academic Emergency Medicine · 2026-09-07 · Added 7 October 2026

Scoping review of 26 studies found EMS performance dashboards are used almost exclusively for routine operations — only one study addressed crisis conditions and it used no dashboard.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Scoping review (PRISMA-ScR)
Participants
26 studies
Main finding
KPIs mapped to the Donabedian structure/process/outcome model with time-based indicators most frequent; 8 of 26 studies (31%) reported dashboards, all in routine operations, and only one study addressed crisis conditions (without a dashboard)
Caveat
Scoping review without formal quality appraisal, drawing on a thin and largely non-crisis literature
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Dashboard maturity varies across Australian services and the review shows the crisis-management evidence base is essentially empty, so this informs system-design discussions rather than current practice.

Learning goal

Understand how performance dashboards in ambulance systems could support decision-making during major incidents and crises.

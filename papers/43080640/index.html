Heat and ozone together raise cardiac arrest risk

Interactions between Ambient Ozone and Heat on Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: A Nationwide Study in Japan
Environment & Health · 2026-07-17 · Added 26 November 2026

Japanese nationwide case-crossover analysis of 610,613 cardiac-origin OHCAs (2012–2019) found ground-level ozone and extreme heat act synergistically, with each 10 ppb ozone rise above a ~60 ppb threshold linked to a 4% higher OHCA risk at extreme heat.

Study type
Case-control
Design
Nationwide time-stratified case-crossover analysis with random-effects meta-pooling
Participants
610,613 cardiac-origin OHCA cases, Japan 2012–2019
Main finding
Above a ~60 ppb ozone threshold, each 10 ppb rise at extreme heat (99th percentile temperature) was associated with RR 1.04 (95% CI 1.03–1.05), with synergistic interaction on both additive and multiplicative scales
Caveat
Exposures came from modelled estimates rather than personal measurement, and individual-level confounders were not assessed
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). The exposure is environmental, not something crews treat — its value is context for surge planning and public-health messaging during Australian heatwaves.

Poor agreement on radiological tension features

Exploring Inter‐Rater Agreement in Classification of Radiological Features of Tension in Adults With Primary Spontaneous Pneumothorax
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 27 July 2026

Interpretation for radiological tension features had poor inter‐rater reliability, which seriously changes their appropriateness as drivers for interventional management.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Blinded observational inter-rater agreement study
Participants
Five emergency physicians reviewing 80 PSP chest x-rays
Main finding
Inter-rater agreement for radiological tension features was Krippendorff's Alpha 0.524 (95% CI 0.400–0.623), indicating poor agreement
Caveat
Small number of x-rays and reviewers; in-hospital ED setting
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the reliability of chest x-ray tension features when deciding whether to decompress a pneumothorax.

EM resident physician response vehicle exposure

Emergency Medicine Residents in the Prehospital Setting: Does This Augment Training?
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 12 May 2026

Five-year retrospective of a physician response vehicle staffed by EM residents found residents gained valuable exposure to adult and paediatric cardiac arrests, refusals, death pronouncements, and critical trauma, with an average of 28-79 encounters per resident.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective observational study of administrative records
Participants
90 EM residents over 5 years
Main finding
Mean encounters per resident ranged from 28.7 (2018) to 79.2 (2022); residents managed 1,313 OHCA (34 paediatric), 1,048 refusals, 596 death pronouncements, 172 critical trauma patients and 2,053 on-line medical control consults.
Caveat
Single program, descriptive only; no educational outcome measures
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how prehospital clinical exposure during specialist training shapes readiness for complex out-of-hospital care.

Mapping anti-violence measures for ambulance personnel

Violence-related measures against ambulance personnel: a study protocol for a scoping review with multi-framework conceptual mapping
HRB Open Research · 2026-07-28 · Added 3 November 2026

An Irish scoping-review protocol will map measures against verbal and physical violence toward ambulance personnel — reported by 57–93% of prehospital responders in a career — across five safety-science and human-factors frameworks.

Prehospital STEMI data transmission speeds reperfusion

Impact of Wechat-based prehospital data transmission on reperfusion times in ST-elevation myocardial infarction: a prospective observational study
Clinics · 2026-09-30 · Added 3 October 2026

In 409 Chinese STEMI patients, prehospital WeChat transmission of patient data to the PCI centre shortened symptom-to-first-medical-contact (75 vs 120 minutes), door-to-balloon (50 vs 67 minutes) and total ischaemic time (121.5 vs 150 minutes).

Study type
Cohort
Design
Prospective observational cohort
Participants
409 STEMI patients (96 with WeChat transmission, 313 without)
Main finding
Median symptom-to-first-medical-contact 75 vs 120 min, door-to-balloon 50 vs 67 min, total ischaemic time 121.5 vs 150 min (all p < 0.05)
Caveat
Non-randomised grouping — patients selected for WeChat transmission may have differed systematically; single-region Chinese system
Bottom line
Supports current practice

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Pre-alerting and transmitting ECGs to cath labs is standard practice here, so this supports what Australian crews already do — the app itself is incidental and the observational design can't separate the effect from selection bias.

Learning goal

Understand how early prehospital notification of STEMI to PCI centres shortens reperfusion times in my practice.

Teen and caregiver views on lethal means counselling

“It’s Just So Important to Make Them Feel Seen”: Teen and Caregiver Perspectives of Lethal Means Safety Planning for the Emergency Department
Annals of Emergency Medicine · Added 27 July 2026

Lethal means safety counseling is an essential, high-yield component of suicide prevention.

Study type
Qualitative
Design
Qualitative semistructured interview study
Participants
33 interviews (19 caregivers, 14 teens) at a tertiary children's hospital ED
Main finding
Themes: lethal means safety counselling helps families feel cared for; tool elements should use technology, equity, plain supportive language and actionable plans; family dynamics and balancing teen autonomy are barriers.
Caveat
Single paediatric ED; participants mostly White, women and English-speaking, limiting generalisability.
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Refine how I deliver lethal means safety counselling with teenagers and their families during mental health presentations.

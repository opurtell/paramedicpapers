Checklists may speed time-critical transfers

Using Checklists to Improve Turnaround Times in Time-Critical Interhospital Transfers
Air Medical Journal · 2026-09 · Added 26 September 2026

A UK HEMS service evaluation found introducing referring-hospital and transport-team checklists for time-critical interhospital helicopter transfers improved achievement of a sub-45-minute turnaround KPI from 52% to 64%, though not significantly.

Study type
Other
Design
Before-after service evaluation with simulation familiarisation
Participants
23 transfers pre-intervention (2022), 11 air transfers post-intervention (2024)
Main finding
Sub-45-minute turnaround achievement rose from 52% to 64% after checklist introduction; the difference was not statistically significant
Caveat
Very small numbers, single organisation, retrospective design, and a helicopter-only cohort
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). A HEMS-specific workflow, but checklist use for time-critical transfers is standard practice across Australian retrieval and road crews, so the finding is relevant despite the tiny numbers.

Learning goal

Understand how checklists can streamline turnaround during time-critical interhospital transfers in my practice.

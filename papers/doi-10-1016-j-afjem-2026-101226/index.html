Dispatch triage overcalls and misses across call types

198. The triage performance of emergency medical dispatch prioritisation compared to prehospital on-scene triage in the Western Cape Province of South Africa
African Journal of Emergency Medicine · 2026-11 · Added 17 November 2026

A South African review of 242,576 emergency dispatches found dispatch priority ratings overtriaged 62% and undertriaged 15% of incidents against scene triage, with the highest undertriage in respiratory, diabetic and chest pain calls.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Retrospective analysis of computer-aided dispatch records and electronic patient care records
Participants
242,576 primary emergency responses
Main finding
Overtriage 62.28% and undertriage 15.29% against the South African Triage Scale; sensitivity 53.71%, specificity 74.31%; highest undertriage in respiratory complaints (31%), diabetes (30%) and chest pain (29%)
Caveat
Single South African dispatch system, compared against scene triage ratings that carry their own error
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Dispatch triage accuracy shapes every ambulance response, and the undertriage pattern in respiratory and chest pain calls is a warning any dispatch centre should read.

Learning goal

Understand the limitations of dispatch-based triage in recognising time-critical respiratory, diabetic and chest pain presentations.

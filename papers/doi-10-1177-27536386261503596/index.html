Turning HEMS debriefs into shared learning

Coffee and cases (C&C) – perceptions of helicopter emergency medical service teams towards a quality improvement project aimed at enhancing organisational learning from team debriefs: A qualitative focus group study
Paramedicine · 2026-11-03 · Added 4 November 2026

Focus groups with 30 HEMS clinicians found a quality improvement project capturing routine debriefs ('Coffee and Cases') strengthened organisational learning through three themes: education, accessibility and calibration of learning points.

Study type
Qualitative
Design
Qualitative descriptive study, four semi-structured focus groups
Participants
30 HEMS clinicians in 4 focus groups
Main finding
Organisational learning was the global theme, organised into education (knowledge generation, engagement, incidental learning), accessibility (learning reaching staff who missed debriefs) and calibration (moderation and vetting of learning points)
Caveat
Single-service perceptions with no measure of whether learning outcomes actually changed
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). It comes from a helicopter crew model, but the underlying problem — debrief learning evaporating unless it's captured and shared — applies to any Australian ambulance service.

Learning goal

Understand how routine team debriefs can be captured and calibrated to strengthen organisational learning in my practice.

Synchronised ventilation faster ROSC in neonatal piglets

Cardiopulmonary resuscitation with synchronized ventilation versus 3:1 compression to ventilation ratio in a neonatal porcine model
Resuscitation Plus · Added 27 July 2026

In a neonatal piglet model of asystolic cardiac arrest, CCSV resulted in a significantly faster time to ROSC compared to 3:1 C:V.

Study type
Simulation
Design
Randomised animal experimental study
Participants
16 neonatal piglets (1-3 days old, 1.8-2.5 kg)
Main finding
Median time to ROSC 61 s (IQR 51-118) with chest compression synchronised ventilation vs 170 s (IQR 105-312) with 3:1 C:V (p=0.030); ROSC rate 7/8 vs 5/8 (p=1.000)
Caveat
Small animal (porcine) model; findings may not translate to human neonatal resuscitation
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the emerging evidence for chest compression synchronised ventilation against standard ratios in neonatal resuscitation.

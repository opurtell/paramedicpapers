Carotid Doppler parameters linked to ROSC

Predictive Value of Carotid Artery Doppler Ultrasound for Resuscitation Outcomes in Patients with Cardiac Arrest
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 11 May 2026

Study type
Cohort
Design
Prospective observational study in one tertiary ED
Participants
51 adults undergoing CPR (23 ROSC, 28 non-ROSC)
Main finding
Carotid Doppler parameters (VTI, Vmax, Vmin, TAV, MD*, RI, dv.MAX) differed significantly between ROSC and non-ROSC groups and predicted ROSC on ROC analysis.
Caveat
Small single-centre sample; Doppler window may have captured ROSC-transition flow; exploratory only
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the usefulness of carotid Doppler parameters for predicting ROSC during resuscitation.

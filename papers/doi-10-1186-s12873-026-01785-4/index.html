On-site CT slashes event transports

Impact of on-site mobile CT imaging on prehospital patient management during the Munich Oktoberfest: a retrospective observational before–after study at a mass gathering
BMC Emergency Medicine · 2026-10-06 · Added 7 October 2026

Retrospective before–after study at the Munich Oktoberfest found an on-site mobile CT led to 429 of 498 imaged patients (86%) being treated and discharged on-site, avoiding an estimated 22,737 minutes of EMS deployment time.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective observational before–after
Participants
498 patients imaged on-site
Main finding
429 of 498 (86%) discharged on-site after imaging; 52 (10%) required hospital transport; 429 EMS transports and ~22,737 minutes of EMS deployment time avoided versus the pre-CT period
Caveat
Retrospective design with a historical (2015–2019) comparator — case-mix and event differences could explain part of the effect
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Low relevance). On-site CT is an in-hospital tool deployed within a physician-staffed European mass-gathering medical model — nothing comparable operates in Australian ambulance practice.

Learning goal

Understand how on-site advanced imaging at mass gatherings can reduce unnecessary hospital transports and resource demands.

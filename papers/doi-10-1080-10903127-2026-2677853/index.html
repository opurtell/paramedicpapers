SCARE score validates for chest pain triage

External Validation of the SCARE Score for Predicting Acute Myocardial Infarction in Patients Calling Emergency Medical Communication Centers: A Prospective Multicenter Study
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 4 June 2026

They prospectively validated the SCARE score for predicting acute myocardial infarction during telephone triage across multiple French emergency communication centers, finding good discrimination and calibration but noting limitations in real-world implementation.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Prospective multicentre observational validation cohort
Participants
2,042 adults hospitalised after EMCC calls for non-traumatic chest pain
Main finding
265 (13.0%) had AMI; SCARE AUROC 0.85 (95% CI 0.82-0.87), AUROC 0.84 in men and 0.89 in women; at >35 points sensitivity 73.6%, specificity 75.3%, NPV 95.0%
Caveat
Score not used in real time and did not influence dispatch; French physician-staffed dispatch centres, so applicability to non-physician dispatch systems untested
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh how structured chest pain telephone triage tools could strengthen my recognition of acute myocardial infarction risk.

AI triage tool predicts ED admission

The Sydney Triage to Admission Risk Tool With Artificial Intelligence (START-AI) to Support Decision Making in Emergency Departments: Model Explainability and Feature Importance Analysis
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 4 August 2026

Model explainability analysis of START-AI — AUROC improved from 0.78 to 0.90 with AI features, driven primarily by blood test results, triage comments, and clinical notes

Study type
Other
Design
Model explainability analysis of single-centre ED electronic medical record data over 2 years
Participants
Not reported
Main finding
AUROC for predicting inpatient admission improved from 0.78 (START alone) to 0.90 with all START-AI features; blood test results (lactate, CRP), triage comments and ED case notes contributed most, vital signs contributed little
Caveat
Single-centre data; prediction of admission rather than patient-centred outcomes
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). transferability — Australian-developed tool with strong validation results; AI triage support is coming to pre-hospital care and this shows what features matter most

Learning goal

Understand how artificial intelligence triage tools using clinical notes and pathology data could support patient flow decisions in Australian emergency care.

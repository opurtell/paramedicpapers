2026 Systematic Review of Evidence-Based Guidelines for Prehospital Care
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 12 September 2026

The 2026 update of the systematic review appraising evidence-based guidelines for prehospital care — abstract not yet available (ahead-of-print), but this recurring review is the reference map of what guideline-level evidence exists in prehospital practice.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Biennial systematic review with NAM and AGREE II appraisal
Participants

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 8 Sep 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Cohort of 724,256 ED presentations across Dutch and Danish hospitals — annual ED volume had no association with in-hospital mortality, while mortality was lower on busier days, driven by excess deaths on quiet ones.

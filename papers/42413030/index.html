ECPR for refractory OHCA in Germany

Landscape of Extracorporeal Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation in Germany. A Nationwide Retrospective Study
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 27 July 2026

Between 2018 and 2023, ECPR was used for 2.9% of refractory OHCA cases admitted to hospitals in Germany.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Nationwide retrospective observational study
Participants
27,056 adults admitted with refractory OHCA; 785 received ECPR
Main finding
ECPR used in 2.9% of cases, rising from 1.8% (2018) to 4.1% (2023); survival 11.3% without vs 11.8% with ECPR (not significant); median 3 annual cases per ECPR centre
Caveat
Retrospective administrative data; no adjustment for selection bias in who received ECPR; German system context
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how patient selection and system capacity shape outcomes when ECPR is considered for refractory out-of-hospital cardiac arrest.

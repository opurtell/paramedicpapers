A US systems approach to MCI triage

Prehospital Triage and Transport During Mass Casualty Incidents and Disasters
The Surgical Clinics of North America · 2026-10-21 · Added 19 November 2026

A US systems review of mass-casualty prehospital triage and transport promotes SALT as the national triage standard, with casualty collection points, ambulance exchange points and incident-command-led transport by a designated transport officer.

Main finding
Sets out early recognition, resource escalation and dynamic primary/secondary/tertiary triage with retriage, casualty collection and ambulance exchange points, and Incident Command System–structured triage-driven transport.
Caveat
A narrative US review reflecting the American MCI framework, not a systematic synthesis of evidence.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). A US tiered MCI framework — Australia runs different national triage tools and command structures, so treat this as a systems comparison for anyone working MCI policy or exercises.

Learning goal

Understand how dynamic retriage and incident command structures shape triage and transport decisions in mass casualty incidents.

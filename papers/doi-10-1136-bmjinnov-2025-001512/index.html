Alfen blind intubation device works on manikins

Alfen: a novel blind intubation device for prehospital medicine – a manikin study
BMJ Innovations · Added 18 May 2026

Manikin study with 157 intubation attempts achieved >98% success rate with the novel blind intubation device across operators of varying experience.

Study type
Simulation
Design
Manikin simulation study
Participants
157 participants across three experience levels using three manikins
Main finding
155 of 157 intubation attempts succeeded (>98%); the two failures were by operators with no prior intubation experience.
Caveat
Manikin study only; no patient data and no comparison with standard laryngoscopy or supraglottic devices.
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the role of novel blind intubation devices against conventional airway techniques in prehospital practice.

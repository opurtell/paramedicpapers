How Utah widened paramedic oxytocin scope

A Community Partnership Initiative in Utah to Enhance Prehospital Perinatal Care in the Prevention of Postpartum Hemorrhage
Journal of Midwifery & Women's Health · 2026-10-22 · Added 24 October 2026

Quality improvement initiative describing how a rural US state changed its EMS protocols in April 2024 to let paramedics give oxytocin to prevent postpartum haemorrhage and AEMTs give it intramuscularly for prevention and treatment.

Study type

Participants

Main finding
State EMS protocols changed in April 2024 to allow paramedic oxytocin for PPH prevention and AEMT intramuscular oxytocin for PPH prevention and treatment
Caveat
Single-state process description with no clinical outcome data
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Oxytocin for postpartum haemorrhage is standard on Australian trucks, but this paper is about how a scope-of-practice change was won, not whether the drug works.

Learning goal

Understand how expanded paramedic scope for oxytocin can strengthen prehospital postpartum haemorrhage care.

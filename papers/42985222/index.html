Low-cost 3D-printed skin tear trainer for paramedics

Development and Initial Assessment of a Low-Cost Skin Tear Management Task Trainer for Paramedic Education
Cureus · 2026-10-05 · Added 6 November 2026

A Canadian team 3D-printed a skin tear management task trainer for paramedic education costing about CAD 35 in materials, and learners rated 14 trainers highly for realism and educational value.

Study type
Simulation
Design
Technical report with iterative prototyping and acceptability assessment
Participants
14 type 1 task trainers assessed with paramedic learners
Main finding
Perceived self-efficacy 4.68/5, realism 4.64/5 and educational value 4.85/5; each unit took 20–30 minutes of active fabrication plus 80–90 minutes curing.
Caveat
Perception ratings only — no objective measure of skill acquisition.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). A Canadian paramedic education project, but low-cost trainers and community wound management are the same challenge here.

Learning goal

Strengthen my wound care technique for skin tears using realistic simulation before managing them in the field.

Paediatric in-hospital arrest trends in Japan

Incidence, outcomes, and temporal trends of pediatric in-hospital cardiac arrest in Japan: a nationwide study
Resuscitation · Added 27 September 2026

Nationwide Japanese administrative data (2011–2022, 7.1M paediatric admissions) testing whether paediatric in-hospital arrest incidence and outcomes are changing — incidence fell from 1.03 to 0.86 per 1000 admissions while survival to discharge rose from 25.8% to 29.6%, with adrenaline given in 71% and intubation in 62% of arrests.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective nationwide administrative database study
Participants
6,962 paediatric in-hospital cardiac arrests among 7,117,481 admissions, 2011–2022
Main finding
Incidence fell from 1.03 to 0.86 per 1000 admissions (p trend 0.009); survival to discharge rose from 25.8% to 29.6% (p trend 0.006); adrenaline given in 71.0% and intubation in 61.7% of arrests
Caveat
Case ascertainment via administrative procedure codes, not a clinical registry
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). in-hospital data, not prehospital, but useful outcome benchmarks for paediatric arrests transported to ED; code-based ascertainment means authors call for registry validation

Learning goal

Update my understanding of how paediatric arrest incidence and survival trends shape resuscitation decisions in prehospital care.

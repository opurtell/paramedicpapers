Insomnia common in paramedic students

Insomnia Severity and Its Demographic and Health-Related Correlates Among Paramedic Students in Saudi Arabia: A Cross-Sectional Study
Healthcare · 2026-09-28 · Added 17 October 2026

A cross-sectional study of 238 Saudi paramedic students found subthreshold insomnia on average (mean ISI 11.8), worse in single students and those at private universities or with chronic or mental illness.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Cross-sectional survey
Participants
238 EMS students
Main finding
Mean Insomnia Severity Index 11.8 ± 5.3; higher in single vs married students (12.1 vs 7.4, p = 0.005) and private vs public university students (19.3 vs 11.6, p = 0.001)
Caveat
Self-report, single country, and no measure of clinical placement or shift exposure
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Saudi paramedic education differs from Australia's, but sleep disturbance in ambulance students mirrors concerns in Australian paramedic cohorts and is a fair reminder that sleep health belongs in training.

Learning goal

Recognise how student and early-career stressors contribute to insomnia across the paramedic workforce.

  • Why ambulance workplace violence occurs

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 26 Aug 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Qualitative study with Swedish ambulance personnel identified three themes driving workplace violence: personnel lack of competence and willingness to adapt, inability to cope with emotional responses to provocation, and limited control in unpredictable situations involving intoxicated or unpredictable patients.

  • Gender gaps in Nordic EMS workplace

    Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · 18 Aug 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Scoping review of gender-related findings in Nordic EMS found women reported higher physical and psychosocial work demands, greater concerns about violence, and higher levels of mental health problems and sleep disturbances compared to male colleagues.

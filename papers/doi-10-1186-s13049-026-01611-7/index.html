Bystander care in first minutes of trauma

The Diamond Minutes: Rethinking the Earliest Link of the Trauma Chain of Survival
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 21 April 2026

Concept editorial arguing that the first minutes after injury, before EMS arrival, are a distinct high-risk window where bystander action and dispatcher guidance can prevent deaths, especially through haemorrhage control and airway management.

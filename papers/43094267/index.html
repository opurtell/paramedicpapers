Experts pick camera angles for dispatcher-guided CPR

Expert-Selected Camera Viewpoints for Dispatcher-Assisted BLS: A Delphi Consensus Study
Journal of Evaluation in Clinical Practice · 2026-12 · Added 27 November 2026

A Spanish modified Delphi study asked 30 resuscitation experts to rank fixed camera viewpoints, simulated as AED-integrated cameras, for dispatcher-assisted BLS — two viewpoints reached strong consensus for breathing assessment, compressions and ventilation.

Study type
Qualitative
Design
Modified Delphi consensus, two online questionnaire rounds
Participants
30 life support experts
Main finding
Strong consensus (≥85%) for two AED-integrated camera viewpoints for breathing assessment, chest compressions and ventilation; consensus on AED-use items was weak (54–77%).
Caveat
Expert-perceived suitability in a simulated setting only — no measured dispatcher or rescuer performance, let alone clinical outcomes.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Expert opinion on simulated footage rather than measured performance, but dispatcher-assisted CPR coaching is the same question for every ambulance service.

Learning goal

Understand how camera-assisted dispatcher guidance shapes the quality of bystander CPR during cardiac arrest.

  • Shockable rhythms decay fast in untreated arrest

    International Emergency Nursing · 26 Nov 2026 · Systematic review · Supports current practice

    Italian-led systematic review of 38 studies (1990–2025, more than 262,700 adult OHCA cases) found shockable rhythms decline progressively with increasing time-to-defibrillation, with earlier shock linked to higher defibrillation success, more ROSC and better survival.

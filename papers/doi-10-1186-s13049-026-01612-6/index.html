Portable telemedicine devices compared for triage

Feasibility of portable telemedicine devices for ambulance-based prehospital triage: a comparative simulation study
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 24 April 2026

Simulation study comparing portable telemedicine devices for ambulance triage, and found the Vuzix headset ranked highest overall, but the smartphone app was the most practical early implementation option.

Study type
Simulation
Design
Comparative simulation study with multi-criteria decision analysis
Participants
47 surveys from ambulance professionals, remote neurologists and a patient actor across four simulated stroke-like scenarios
Main finding
Vuzix head-mounted device ranked highest (value score 0.80), ahead of Siilo smartphone app (0.77) and RealWear (0.73); ambulance crews favoured smartphone usability, neurologists favoured Vuzix video quality
Caveat
Simulation setting only; no clinical or operational impact assessed
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Understand how portable telemedicine devices could support prehospital stroke triage within Australian ambulance systems.

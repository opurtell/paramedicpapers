AI dispatcher copilot concept for Tele-CPR

The AI dispatcher copilot: beyond cardiac arrest recognition to dynamic large language model-assisted Tele-CPR
Resuscitation · Added 29 July 2026

Concept paper proposes an AI dispatcher copilot using multimodal large language models for dynamic Tele-CPR, integrating acoustic agonal breathing detection, adaptive instruction generation, real-time video CPR coaching with compression feedback, and automated first responder and AED coordination.

Proposes an LLM-enabled 'AI dispatcher copilot' integrating agonal breathing detection, adaptive instruction generation, video CPR coaching and automated first responder/AED coordination, aligned with ERC 2025 guidelines.
Caveat

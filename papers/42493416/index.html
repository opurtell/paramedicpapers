Diphtheria re-emergence and ED impact

The Return of Diphtheria: Reflections From the Emergency Department
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 27 July 2026

ABSTRACT Diphtheria has re‐emerged in Australia in 2026, prompting significant public health concern and placing additional demands on healthcare services.

Study type
Other

Descriptive review of diphtheria outbreak impact on EDs
Participants

Main finding
Diphtheria disproportionately affected Indigenous communities; ED challenges included case identification, rapid guideline development, broader cutaneous presentations and workforce strain from contact tracing and staff isolation

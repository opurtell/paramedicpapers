Pregnancy and return-to-work guidance for prehospital staff

FPHC Wellbeing: pregnancy, maternity and return-to-work guidelines
British Paramedic Journal · 2026-09-01 · Added 29 August 2026

UK Faculty of Pre-hospital Care guidelines on pregnancy, maternity and return to work — every prehospital organisation should run individualised risk assessments, support maternal choice and offer personalised return-to-work programmes.

Study type
Guideline / statement
Design
Guideline / consensus statement (questionnaire, focus groups and literature review)
Participants
Not reported
Main finding
Recommendations: all organisations need maternity and parental-leave policies, individualised risk assessments incorporating maternal preferences, personalised return-to-work programmes, and compliance with breastfeeding-at-work legal requirements.
Caveat
UK legal framework and voluntary-sector-heavy consultation participants; no Australian equivalence assessment.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). UK faculty guidance rather than Australian policy, but the risk-assessment and return-to-work principles map directly onto Australian ambulance employers' obligations.

Learning goal

Understand how individualised risk assessment and personalised return-to-work planning support pregnant paramedics in Australia.

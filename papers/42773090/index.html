AI triage of ED CT brain scans

Artificial Intelligence Triage of Urgent Versus Non‐Urgent CT Brain Findings to Support Expedited Emergency Department Disposition: A Retrospective Validation Study
Emergency Medicine Australasia · Added 29 September 2026

Retrospective validation of an AI model classifying 3,424 ED CT brain scans as urgent or not — 98.3% negative predictive value with no false negative needing urgent intervention at the index visit, putting ~64% of scans in the possibly-expedite bucket pending a prospective trial.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Retrospective validation study of an AI model
Participants
3,424 adult non-contrast ED CT brain scans
Main finding
AI sensitivity 85.4% (95% CI 80.0–89.0%), specificity 69.1%, NPV 98.3%; post hoc re-adjudication gave sensitivity 94.3% and NPV 99.4%; ~64% of encounters potentially eligible for expedited disposition; no false negative required urgent intervention at index visit.
Caveat
Retrospective single-centre design with reference standard from radiologist reports; needs prospective validation.
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Low relevance). transferability — hospital imaging workflow with no direct application to ACTAS practice; context for ED flow and ramping pressure

Learning goal

Understand how AI-supported triage of CT brain findings could shape safe expedited emergency department disposition decisions.

Untangling trauma terms for first responders

Toward a Definition and Differentiation of Secondary Traumatic Stress, Vicarious Trauma, Post-Traumatic Stress, and Compassion Fatigue in Relation to First Responders: An Integrative Literature Review
Trauma, Violence & Abuse · 2026-11-13 · Added 14 November 2026

A French integrative review of 76 studies on first responders (including paramedics) found the concepts of secondary traumatic stress, vicarious trauma, compassion fatigue and PTSD overlap symptomatically, with organisational constraints playing a central role in traumatic responses.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Integrative literature review of 76 studies across six databases to January 2026
Participants
76 studies of adult first responders (firefighters, police, paramedics, ambulance workers)
Main finding
Conceptual overlap between secondary traumatic stress, vicarious trauma, compassion fatigue and PTSD is maintained by symptomatic overlap and measurement tools designed for other professions; organisational constraints emerged as central to trauma emergence
Caveat
Conceptual synthesis with no intervention data, and heterogeneous included professions
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Workforce psychological injury is a universal ambulance problem, and the review's emphasis on organisational drivers rather than individual resilience speaks directly to Australian services.

Learning goal

Understand how secondary traumatic stress, vicarious trauma and compassion fatigue differ and how organisational constraints shape my wellbeing.

Paediatric non-transport re-call rates low

Repeat 9-1-1 Calls and Clinical Outcomes of Children After Non-Transport by Emergency Medical Services
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 29 September 2026

US retrospective study of 1.85 million paediatric ambulance calls (40% not transported) — only 1% of non-transported children re-called and were transported within 72 hours and deaths were rare (0.1% dead on scene at re-call), with under-5s, neurologic impressions and disadvantaged areas over-represented among re-calls.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Retrospective cross-sectional study of EMS data
Participants
1,849,925 paediatric 9-1-1 calls (732,525 not transported)
Main finding
1% of non-transported children had a repeat 9-1-1 call with transport within 72 hours; 11 (0.1%) deaths on scene at re-call; of 1,001 with hospital-linked data, 89% ED discharge, 16% hospitalised, 0.2% died.
Caveat
US retrospective data with only one third of records hospital-linked; not generalisable to Australian systems.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). transferability — no formulary or skill dependency, speaks directly to the leave-at-home decision every AP makes; US follow-up access differences mean exact percentages won't transplant

Learning goal

Recognise the risk factors that should prompt closer safety-netting when a child is not transported.

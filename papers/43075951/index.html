Paragliding festival injuries cluster at landing

Transition-Phase Injuries and Wilderness EMS Operations During a Mountain Paragliding Festival: A Brief Report
Wilderness & Environmental Medicine · 2026-11-24 · Added 25 November 2026

A Turkish brief report of wilderness EMS operations at a 2-day mountain paragliding festival recorded 13 injuries across 4716 flights, with more than three-quarters occurring during landing.

Study type
Case series
Design
Retrospective review of operational and prehospital records from a 2-day mountain paragliding festival (brief report)
Participants
13 injuries across 4716 flights
Main finding
10 of 13 injuries (76.9%) occurred during landing versus 1 during takeoff; lower-limb trauma predominated; 8 patients went by ground ambulance and 1 deceased pilot required helicopter-assisted recovery.
Caveat
Single 2-day event, small numbers, retrospective operational records.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Terrain-aware event standby and rescue planning translate directly to festival, alpine and flight-sport coverage Australian crews provide.

Learning goal

Recognise landing-phase injury patterns and terrain risks when planning prehospital coverage for mass aviation events.

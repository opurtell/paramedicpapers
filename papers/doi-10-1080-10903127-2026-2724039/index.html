Campus EMS naloxone and harm reduction survey

Harm Reduction on Campus: A National Survey of Collegiate-based EMS Agencies
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 25 August 2026

National survey examined harm reduction programs in collegiate-based EMS agencies, assessing naloxone distribution, overdose response, and substance use support services.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Cross-sectional online survey of collegiate EMS agency leaders
Participants
41 collegiate-based EMS agencies (33% response rate)
Main finding
93% stocked naloxone for overdose response, 46% offered public naloxone education, 7% distributed fentanyl test strips; barriers were institutional/policy issues, community engagement challenges, and resource constraints
Caveat
Low response rate and US collegiate EMS setting, limiting generalisability
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). transferability — US campus-specific context but harm reduction principles applicable to community paramedicine models

Learning goal

Understand the system barriers that limit naloxone distribution and harm reduction in first response settings.

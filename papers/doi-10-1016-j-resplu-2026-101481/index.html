CONOCES-BLS scale adapted for citizens

Adaptation of the CONOCES-BLS scale to citizens: evidence of content validity and preliminary psychometric evaluation
Resuscitation Plus · Added 20 September 2026

Spanish methodological study adapting the CONOCES-BLS knowledge scale for adult laypersons via Delphi expert consensus and pilot testing — content validity was strong (expert agreement above 0.90 on every item, Krippendorff coefficients 0.91–1.0) and internal consistency adequate (Cronbach's alpha 0.82), supporting its use for assessing community BLS knowledge pending further psychometric work.

  • Knowledge gaps in local anaesthetic dosing and LAST

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 15 Sep 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    Multicentre survey of 114 emergency clinicians across three Queensland EDs — fewer than half correctly identified the recommended maximum dose for any of four local anaesthetics, 21.9% couldn't recall any features of LAST, and only 10.5% could state the lipid emulsion dosing regimen.

  • Kigali mass casualty training lifts knowledge

    African Journal of Emergency Medicine · 11 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Quasi-experimental pre–post study of a two-part mass casualty training curriculum (asynchronous video modules plus a half-day simulation) for 41 emergency providers and community first responders in Kigali — mean knowledge scores rose significantly after training and remained above baseline at 3- and 6-month follow-up, with significant confidence gains across key MCI skills.

  • VR adds little to CPR simulation training

    Nurse Education Today · 10 Sep 2026 · RCT · Context only

    RCT of 246 undergraduate nursing students testing immersive VR plus manikin simulation against manikin simulation alone for CPR group training — no significant difference in self-confidence or CPR group performance after cluster adjustment, though students rated VR higher on satisfaction and usability, and 33.6% reported dizziness and 19% headaches from headsets.

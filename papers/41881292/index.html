Pacing artefact can mask VF during TCP

Delayed Defibrillation of Ventricular Arrhythmias During Transcutaneous Pacing
Resuscitation · Added 21 April 2026

Case series examining the problem of ventricular arrhythmias occurring during TCP and delays to defibrillation — highlights the clinical trap of pacing artefact masking VF and the need to pause pacing to identify shockable rhythms.

Online medical direction mostly for refusals

Patterns of Online Medical Direction Utilization in an Urban EMS System: A Retrospective Analysis of 9,944 Consultations
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 18 August 2026

Retrospective analysis of 9,944 online medical direction consultations from 2016-2024 found OLMD is primarily used for patient refusals (65%) with 97% physician approval, and overall ~93% agreement between paramedic requests and physician decisions

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective descriptive analysis of consultation records
Participants
9,944 online medical direction consultations (2016-2024)
Main finding
Patient refusals accounted for 65% (n=5,175) of consultations with 97% physician approval; termination of resuscitation and transport destination requests approved in 98% and 90%; overall physician agreement ~93%
Caveat
Single urban US (border) EMS system, may not generalise to Australian practice
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). transferability — Large real-world EMS dataset directly applicable to OLMD practice patterns and protocol refinement

Learning goal

Understand the system factors that shape when paramedics seek online medical direction for patient refusals and capacity decisions.

No evidence to change ALS in toxic arrest

Intra-arrest strategies to treat cardiac arrest due to a non-opioid toxicological causes in adults and children: a systematic review
Resuscitation Plus · 2026-11 · Added 25 September 2026

A systematic review of intra-arrest treatment modifications for toxicological cardiac arrest found only four observational studies, all at critical risk of bias, with no evidence supporting any change to standard advanced life support.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Systematic review (GRADE-assessed)
Participants
4 observational studies, only one over 100 patients
Main finding
The largest study (1,545 patients) found bicarbonate in suspected-overdose OHCA was associated with lower survival to discharge (aOR 0.16, 95% CI 0.08–0.31)
Caveat
All included studies at critical risk of bias, none in children, estimates very imprecise
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). The question is squarely one for ambulance crews — whether to alter standard ALS for suspected overdose arrest — and sodium bicarbonate is carried across Australian services.

Learning goal

Weigh the evidence for bicarbonate use during cardiac arrest of suspected toxicological cause in adults.

Mobile ECPR team cannulates on scene

Prehospital extracorporeal cardiopulmonary resuscitation for refractory out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: a protocol for the Rapid prehospital ACcess to ECPR in Prague (RACE Prague) single-arm pilot feasibility study
Resuscitation Plus · 2026-09-24 · Added 19 October 2026

Protocol for a single-arm pilot dispatching a mobile ECPR team to cannulate refractory OHCA patients at the scene, aiming to cut collapse-to-flow from the ~60 minutes typical of in-hospital ECPR by at least 15 minutes.

Study type
Other
Design
Single-arm pilot feasibility trial protocol
Participants
20 adults with witnessed refractory OHCA
Main finding
Primary endpoint is collapse-to-ECMO flow time (target ≥15 min shorter than in-hospital ECPR), tested against historical and contemporaneous in-hospital cohorts; results not yet available
Caveat
Single-centre, single-arm feasibility design with no randomised comparison.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Low relevance). A physician-staffed mobile cannulation team is a highly specialised model that doesn't map onto Australian ambulance systems; worth watching as the prehospital ECPR evidence builds.

Learning goal

Weigh the practicality and potential benefit of prehospital ECPR cannulation for refractory out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in Australia.

Post-ROSC signs of life not pre-ROSC prognostic

Post-ROSC Signs of Life Are Not Pre-ROSC Prognostic Markers
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 25 August 2026

Letter to Editor argued post-ROSC signs of life should not be used as pre-ROSC prognostic markers, emphasising that clinical signs after ROSC do not predict outcomes during arrest.

Study type
Other
Design
Letter to the editor responding to a descriptive study of signs of life in EMS-treated OHCA
Participants
617 EMS-treated OHCA cases discussed (44 with at least one sign of life)
Main finding
Argues that signs of life observed after ROSC should not be used as pre-ROSC prognostic markers during arrest
Caveat
Letter/commentary, not original data
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). transferability — Clarifies prognostication timing — important for transport and continuation-of-resuscitation decisions

Learning goal

Refine my judgement on when to continue resuscitation by separating prognostic signs seen during arrest from those appearing after ROSC.

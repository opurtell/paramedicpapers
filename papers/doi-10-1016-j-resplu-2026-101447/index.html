Smartwatch fall detection witnesses OHCA

Wearable saving lives: a case report
Resuscitation Plus · Added 26 August 2026

Case report demonstrating that smartwatch fall detection can serve as a digital witness in unwitnessed out-of-hospital cardiac arrest, triggering alerts that enabled timely CPR initiation and resulted in favourable neurological recovery.

Study type
Case series
Design
Case report
Participants
1 man, 69, with unwitnessed OHCA while running
Main finding
Smartwatch fall detection triggered automated alerts; CPR started ~6-7 min after collapse; VF was initial rhythm, ROSC achieved, discharged day 29 with complete neurological recovery
Caveat
Single case; no evidence of generalisable benefit
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Commercial wearable technology is widely available in the community, and EMS personnel increasingly encounter patients with smartwatches triggering alerts — understanding this workflow improves response coordination and outcomes.

Learning goal

Build my understanding of how wearable alert technology can support earlier bystander response to unwitnessed cardiac arrest.

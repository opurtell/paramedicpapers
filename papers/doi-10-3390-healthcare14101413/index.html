AED use varies by race and geography

Racial and Geographic Disparities in Automated External Defibrillator Use During EMS Encounters in the United States
Healthcare · Added 21 May 2026

Analysis of AED deployment patterns during EMS encounters revealing racial and geographic differences in defibrillator access and application.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
Descriptive cross-sectional analysis of aggregated national EMS data
Participants
106,246 EMS encounters across six racial/ethnic groups
Main finding
AED applied in 16,688 encounters (15.7%); rates varied by race and geography, with lowest use in White patients and markedly lower use in suburban and frontier areas
Caveat
Aggregated public data cannot distinguish true inequity from case or system factors
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how community access barriers affect early defibrillator use in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest.

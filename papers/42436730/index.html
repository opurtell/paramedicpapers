Timing of out-of-hospital traumatic cardiac arrest

Timing and outcomes of out-of-hospital traumatic cardiac arrest: results of a multicentre, retrospective cohort study
Resuscitation Plus · Added 27 July 2026

Nine out of ten out-of-hospital TCAs occur before EMS arrival.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Multicentre retrospective observational cohort (nationwide HEMS)
Participants
1,532 patients with out-of-hospital traumatic cardiac arrest
Main finding
90.2% sustained TCA before EMS arrival, 8.2% after EMS arrival and 1.6% during HEMS transport; sustained ROSC occurred in 20.8%, 47.2% and 92.0% respectively; meaningful survival achievable regardless of TCA onset
Caveat
Retrospective design; HEMS-attended patients only, limiting generalisability to routine ambulance responses
Bottom line
Supports current practice

Learning goal

Weigh the prognostic value of arrest timing during transport when deciding duration of resuscitation in traumatic cardiac arrest.

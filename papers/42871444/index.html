Situational judgement tests in paramedic hiring

The Use of Situational Judgement Tests for Paramedic Selection in Canada: A National Cross-Sectional Survey
Cureus · 2026-09-13 · Added 15 October 2026

National survey of 21 Canadian paramedic services found situational judgement tests and multiple mini-interviews are rarely used in frontline paramedic hiring, mostly by smaller services despite the tools being designed for mass testing.

Study type
Cross-sectional
Design
National cross-sectional survey
Participants
21 paramedic services
Main finding
Neither SJTs nor MMIs widely used in Canadian paramedic hiring; only smaller services reported using them
Caveat
21 complete responses — low participation limits representativeness
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Canadian hiring structures differ, but how services assess interpersonal and cognitive skills in applicants is a live question for Australian services too.

Learning goal

Understand how situational judgement assessment could strengthen recruitment and selection of paramedics nationally.

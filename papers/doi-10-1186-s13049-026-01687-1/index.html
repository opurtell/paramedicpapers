ED volume not linked to mortality

The association of annual and daily volume with in-hospital mortality in Dutch and Danish Emergency Departments
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 8 September 2026

Cohort of 724,256 ED presentations across Dutch and Danish hospitals — annual ED volume had no association with in-hospital mortality, while mortality was lower on busier days, driven by excess deaths on quiet ones.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Retrospective international multicentre cohort with mixed-effects logistic regression
Participants
724,256 ED visits (486,558 Dutch, 237,698 Danish) across 12 EDs
Main finding
Annual ED volume showed no association with in-hospital mortality; busier relative daily volume was associated with lower mortality in the Netherlands (e.g. dyspnoea AOR 0.89, 95% CI 0.86-0.92), driven by excess deaths on low-volume days
Caveat
Only 12 EDs in two European countries; adjusted observational associations cannot establish causation
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (Low relevance). transferability — in-hospital systems finding with no direct road application

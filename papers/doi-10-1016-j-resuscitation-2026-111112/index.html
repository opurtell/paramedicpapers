    Resuscitation · 29 Jul 2026 · RCT · Hypothesis-generating

    Cluster-randomized trial of 108 OHCA patients compared video-assisted dispatcher CPR instruction with conventional telephone instruction, finding no difference in bystander CPR rates (87.3% vs 92.5%, p=0.53) or ongoing CPR at EMS arrival, but significantly lower protocol compliance in the telemedicine group.

  • Conventional CPR best for drowning OHCA

    Resuscitation · 29 Jul 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Retrospective cohort of 3914 drowning-related OHCA patients found conventional CPR (compressions with rescue breaths) was associated with higher 1-month survival compared with hands-only CPR (24.1% vs 9.4%), and both were superior to no CPR (2.7%).

Few quality indicators for prehospital post-ROSC care

Identifying Quality Indicators for Prehospital Care Following Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: A Focused Scoping Review
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 25 August 2026

Scoping review identified 16 potential quality indicators for prehospital post-ROSC care across five domains, highlighting a major evidence gap since only two existing studies listed formal indicators.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Focused scoping review (PRISMA-guided)
Participants
46 studies included from 337 records
Main finding
Only 2 included studies listed formal quality indicators; 16 potential prehospital post-ROSC quality indicators were extracted across five domains
Caveat
Most included studies were in-hospital; scoping review without formal consensus yet
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). transferability — Directly addresses prehospital practice gap — quality indicators could standardise post-ROSC care and benchmark performance

Learning goal

Understand how quality indicators for prehospital post-ROSC care can strengthen systems-level cardiac arrest practice.

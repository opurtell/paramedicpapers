Over-rapid BP drop worse after ICH

Early Intensive Blood Pressure Reduction After Intracerebral Hemorrhage Is Associated With Worse Functional Outcome: The Risk of Overshooting Blood Pressure Goals
Annals of Emergency Medicine · Added 27 July 2026

Retrospective cohort examining whether rapid systolic BP reduction ≤150 mm Hg after spontaneous intracerebral haemorrhage improved outcomes — early target achievement and overshooting <120 mm Hg were associated with worse functional outcome.

Study type
Cohort
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Learning goal

Weigh the benefits and risks of rapid systolic blood pressure lowering when managing suspected intracerebral haemorrhage.

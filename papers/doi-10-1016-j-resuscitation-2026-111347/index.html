Paediatric OHCA in Australia and NZ: Aus-ROC report

The Epidemiology of Paediatric Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest in Australia and New Zealand: A Report from The Australasian Resuscitation Outcomes Consortium Epistry (Aus-ROC)
Resuscitation · 2026-10 · Added 10 October 2026

The first Australasian registry study of paediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrest across Australia and New Zealand (4779 EMS-attended cases) found 30-day/hospital discharge survival of 10.0% in EMS-treated cases, with large variation by age group and survival linked to bystander-witnessed arrest, initial shockable rhythm, aetiology and EMS response time.

Study type
Registry
Design
Prospective multicentre registry cohort (Aus-ROC Epistry)
Participants
4779 EMS-attended paediatric OHCA cases (3139 EMS-treated)
Main finding
Incidence 10.1 per 100,000 (EMS-attended) and 6.6 (EMS-treated); bimodal age peak in infants (65.4) and adolescents (11.4); 66.9% unwitnessed, 91.1% non-shockable initial rhythm, 74.3% at home, 62.7% bystander CPR, 1.6% bystander defibrillation; survival 10.0% overall (15.4% late childhood, 7.9% infants); older age, witnessed arrest, shockable rhythm, aetiology and EMS response time associated with survival.
Caveat
Registry cohort design, so associations cannot establish causation, and completeness of bystander interventions and outcome data may vary across jurisdictions.
Bottom line
Context only

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Provides the first region-wide baseline epidemiology of paediatric OHCA, directly relevant to Australian paramedic practice, community CPR/defibrillation initiatives, and age-specific resuscitation and prevention strategies.

Learning goal

Understand the epidemiology of paediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in Australia to inform my resuscitation practice.

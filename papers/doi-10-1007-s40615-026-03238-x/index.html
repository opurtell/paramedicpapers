Race, Gender, and Survival: a Scoping Review of Disparities in ICU Post-Resuscitation Care and Neurological Outcomes Following Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest

Race, Gender, and Survival: a Scoping Review of Disparities in ICU Post-Resuscitation Care and Neurological Outcomes Following Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest
Journal of Racial and Ethnic Health Disparities · Added 20 October 2026

Scoping review of 15 studies found Black women in the US received less bystander CPR, slower emergency response, less ICU intervention and more early DNR/withdrawal decisions after OHCA, with the disparities compounding across the chain of survival.

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). The equity mechanics — bystander CPR gaps and treatment-withdrawal patterns — are a useful mirror for any service auditing its own outcomes data, though the US race-specific findings don't transfer directly to Australian population groups.

Learning goal

Recognise how patient demographics may shape resuscitation care and withdrawal decisions in my own practice.

Tactical police medics trained but underused

The role of tactical police medics in the prehospital environment: results from an interview study
Scandinavian Journal of Trauma, Resuscitation and Emergency Medicine · Added 6 May 2026

Interview study exploring how Swedish tactical police medics view their training, role, and real-world practice — they reported being well trained but underused, with scope restrictions and inconsistent collaboration limiting what they can actually do.

Study type
Qualitative
Design
Qualitative semi-structured interview study
Participants
13 Swedish tactical police medics
Main finding
Medics were satisfied with training but reported a mismatch between training and actual tasks, restrictions preventing care for third parties, and inconsistent collaboration with ambulance services.
Caveat
Small single-country qualitative sample, not generalisable
Bottom line
Context only

Learning goal

Understand how role clarity and interagency collaboration shape prehospital care in tactical and high-risk police operations.

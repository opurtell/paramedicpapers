Ten Second Triage review: fast, promising, unproven

Evaluating the Role of Ten Second Triage in Mass‐Casualty Incidents: A Rapid Review of Early Major‐Incident Triage Tools
Emergency Medicine Australasia · 2026-11-25 · Added 27 November 2026

An Australian-led rapid review of 38 studies and 30 mass-casualty triage tools assessed whether Ten Second Triage works as an early screening tool — no tool consistently won, but TST showed 80.3% accuracy at roughly 10 seconds per casualty.

Study type
Systematic review
Design
Rapid review (Cochrane Rapid Review guidance, PRISMA 2020, PROSPERO-registered)
Participants
38 studies evaluating 30 triage tools
Main finding
No single triage system consistently outperformed others; reported accuracy ranged from 42.4% to 94.2%, with TST at 80.3% accuracy, ~10 seconds per casualty and less reliance on physiological measurements.
Caveat
Marked heterogeneity in study designs and outcome measures, and no evaluation of TST in real mass-casualty environments.
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). MCI triage is core business for every Australian service, but this is a rapid review over heterogeneous evidence with no real-world MCI outcome data behind it.

Learning goal

Strengthen my judgement on selecting and applying rapid triage tools in Australian mass-casualty response.

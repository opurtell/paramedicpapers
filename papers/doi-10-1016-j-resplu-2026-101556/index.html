  • Mainstream and sidestream capnography give different ETCO2

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    A Spanish retrospective analysis of 23 OHCA recordings with concurrent mainstream and sidestream capnography found mainstream systematically recorded higher ETCO2 (bias 3.94 mmHg, limits of agreement -3.21 to 11.09 mmHg), though trends and abrupt rises/drops were consistently co-detected.

  • Community first responders trained but rarely dispatched

    Resuscitation Plus · 8 Nov 2026 · Cross-sectional · Hypothesis-generating

    A cross-sectional survey of 2,704 registered community first responders in two Swedish regions before local system launch found most were CPR-trained and felt prepared (97.3%) but real dispatch experience was low (15.1% ever dispatched, 1.9% performed CPR).

  • Long-term life after ECPR for cardiac arrest

    Resuscitation Plus · 6 Nov 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    An Italian tertiary centre followed refractory OHCA survivors treated with ECPR (63 of 406 discharged alive) and their caregivers, finding long-term survival largely confined to good neurological recovery but preserved quality of life, with cognitive impairment and sleep problems common and anxiety more frequent in caregivers.

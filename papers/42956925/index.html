AI ECG model excludes ACS after cardiac arrest

Artificial Intelligence-Assisted, Electrocardiogram-Derived, Sequential Rule-Based Decision Tree for Exclusion of Acute Coronary Syndrome as the Etiology of Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest: A Single-Center Retrospective Cohort Study
Resuscitation · 2026-10 · Added 31 October 2026

A South Korean single-centre study built an AI-assisted ECG decision tree that classified about half of post-OHCA patients as 'ACS not likely' with no adjudicated ACS case misclassified in development or temporal validation.

Study type
Cohort
Design
Single-centre retrospective cohort with temporal validation
Participants
400 adults with post-ROSC OHCA (329 development / 71 validation)
Main finding
The tree labelled 52.0% of development and 45.1% of validation patients 'ACS not likely' with zero missed ACS cases; yield concentrated in non-shockable rhythms (64.8% of non-ACS cases excluded, AUROC 0.824)
Caveat
Single centre and retrospective, using a deep-learning ECG platform nowhere near ambulance deployment
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (Medium relevance). Cath-lab activation after ROSC is a triage decision Australian crews feed into, and this tool needs multicentre prospective validation before it leaves the research setting.

Learning goal

Weigh the role of AI-assisted ECG interpretation in guiding post-ROSC destination and cath lab decisions.

Risk scores sort survival odds in paediatric OHCA

Predicting survival in pediatric out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: Development and internal validation of risk scores
The American Journal of Emergency Medicine · 2026-10-31 · Added 12 November 2026

A US national registry study (CARES, 2013–2023) developed and internally validated paediatric OHCA risk scores from prehospital variables, classifying 26,895 children into low/moderate/high survival groups with AUROC 0.76–0.82 and average discharge-survival probabilities from 3% to 48%.

Study type
Registry
Design
Registry-based risk-score development and internal validation
Participants
26,895 paediatric OHCA patients
Main finding
27.8% survived to admission and 11.9% to discharge; scores using gender, arrest location, witness status, aetiology, bystander CPR, prehospital AED use and first rhythm reached AUROC 0.76–0.82, with average survival 3–48% across low/moderate/high groups
Caveat
Internal validation only — not yet externally validated or prospectively tested at the bedside
Bottom line
Hypothesis-generating

Why it matters in Australia (High relevance). Built from a 26,895-patient national arrest registry using variables crews already record — bystander CPR, AED use, first rhythm — so the score travels well beyond the US.

Developing an Emergency Medical Services Research Reading List for Emergency Medical Services Practitioners
Prehospital Emergency Care · Added 27 July 2026

We identified 10 high-impact primary publications to prioritize for inclusion in initial and ongoing EMS education to inform evidenced-based out-of-hospital care and address gaps in existing prehospital EBGs.

Study type
Other
Design
Structured expert-panel consensus rating of submitted publications
Participants
22 publications rated by 9 EMS experts
Main finding
10 primary articles rated ≥3.56 average importance were selected as a priority reading list for EMS education
Caveat
Small expert panel; submissions limited to 2021-2023 and to consortium networks
Bottom line
Context only

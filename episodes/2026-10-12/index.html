  • Bedside observations beat NEWS for spotting deterioration

    Resuscitation Plus · 12 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective analysis of 4,581 rapid response team callouts testing whether eight equipment-free bedside observations identify dying patients better than NEWS — a model built from them outperformed NEWS for predicting death within 24 hours (AUC 0.790 vs 0.707).

  • Stable penetrating chest trauma: surgery increasingly avoided

    BMC Emergency Medicine · 12 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Ten-year retrospective review of 268 haemodynamically stable penetrating chest injuries in Marseille, where non-operative management was attempted in 78% and failed in fewer than 9%, with hospital stay falling from 7.6 to 5.2 days in the recent period.

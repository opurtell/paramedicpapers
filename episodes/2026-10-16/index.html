Papers in this episode

  • Signs of life in traumatic OHCA

    The Journal of Emergency Medicine · 16 Oct 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective analysis of 462 adults with traumatic OHCA from a Japanese prospective registry found 4.8% survived beyond the ED, with the best survival where scene cardiac activity and spontaneous respiration coexisted (25%).

  • Prehospital pain assessment happens too rarely

    Medical Journal of the Islamic Republic of Iran · 16 Oct 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Systematic review of eight prehospital observational studies found pain was assessed and documented in only 22.5% to 46.7% of patients, well below recommended standards.

  • Delays in NSW paediatric trauma retrieval

    Air Medical Journal · 16 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Ten-year review of paediatric trauma retrievals by NSW's specialist paediatric transport service found helicopter launch times worsened after 2017 operational changes and teams took over 10 minutes to depart the referring hospital in 80% of missions.

  • One in five transports: brief stay, no intervention

    BMJ Open · 16 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Danish population-based cohort of 89,560 emergency calls found 19.1% of transported patients had a hospital stay of six hours or less with no documented in-hospital intervention, with short-term mortality of just 0.4% in that group.

Papers in this episode

  • Feedback device lifts paediatric ventilation quality

    Pediatrics · 21 Oct 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Randomised crossover simulation study with 123 EMS clinicians testing a ventilation feedback device on a paediatric manikin — guideline-adherent tidal volumes jumped from 16.2% to 87% of breaths with feedback, and rate adherence from 65% to 88.6%.

  • TBI in first responders needs occupational pathways

    The Journal of Head Trauma Rehabilitation · 21 Oct 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Introduction to a themed issue on TBI in first responders, finding a substantial but underrecognised burden with no consistent pathways for recognition, rehabilitation or return to duty, and presenting a 28-recommendation multidisciplinary consensus statement.

  • Joint training lifts behavioural emergency confidence

    BMC Medical Education · 21 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    City-wide single-group pre-post evaluation of a 4-hour interprofessional behavioural-emergency course in Taiwan — self-efficacy rose from 3.16 to 3.54 (dz 0.76) and cognition improved in the 395 paired attendees, but stigma scores did not improve.

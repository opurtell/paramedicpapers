Papers in this episode

  • Cancer markedly raises OHCA risk

    JACC. Advances · 7 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Swedish matched cohort of ~800,000 cancer patients and 3.7 million controls found cancer carried a 55% higher hazard of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (aHR 1.55), with the greatest short-term excess in lung and neurological cancers.

  • Prehospital RSI: what makes it hard

    Health SA = SA Gesondheid · 7 Oct 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Qualitative focus-group study of 39 South African emergency care practitioners found pre-hospital RSI is hampered by system-driven administrative demands, resource and workforce constraints, and communication gaps affecting clinical decision-making.

  • EMS dashboards: thin crisis evidence

    Archives of Academic Emergency Medicine · 7 Oct 2026 · Systematic review · Context only

    Scoping review of 26 studies found EMS performance dashboards are used almost exclusively for routine operations — only one study addressed crisis conditions and it used no dashboard.

  • On-site CT slashes event transports

    BMC Emergency Medicine · 7 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Context only

    Retrospective before–after study at the Munich Oktoberfest found an on-site mobile CT led to 429 of 498 imaged patients (86%) being treated and discharged on-site, avoiding an estimated 22,737 minutes of EMS deployment time.

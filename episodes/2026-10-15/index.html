  • Dispatcher CPR and survival in shockable OHCA

    Physiology International · 15 Oct 2026 · Registry · Supports current practice

    Nationwide Hungarian cohort of 3,674 shockable OHCAs found dispatcher-assisted CPR ongoing at ambulance arrival was associated with far better outcomes than crew-initiated CPR — 30-day survival 54.6% vs 7.1% and one-year survival 45.8% vs 6.2%.

  • EMS sepsis suspicion speeds up ED antibiotics

    Wiener Klinische Wochenschrift · 15 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Single-centre Austrian cohort of 134 ED sepsis admissions found EMS documentation of sepsis suspicion was linked to antibiotics within 1 hour in 63.3% vs 19.2% of patients (aOR 5.77) and a median time-to-antibiotics of 32 vs 124.5 minutes.

  • Cardiogenic shock: prehospital recognition and routing

    US Cardiology · 15 Oct 2026 · Guideline / statement · Context only

    Best-practice review of cardiogenic shock systems of care covering prehospital and ED evaluation argues early identification and direct routing to shock centres prevents irreversible organ damage, while conceding prehospital identification remains difficult.

Papers in this episode

  • Five scene findings flag kids needing blood

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 19 Oct 2026 · Registry · Hypothesis-generating

    Retrospective national trauma registry analysis testing five EMS-observable findings for spotting injured children who need early blood — altered GCS (aOR 8.38), penetrating mechanism (aOR 5.87) and age-adjusted hypotension (aOR 5.39) led, and a three-tier decision tree put 36.8% of early transfusions into just 3.3% of kids.

  • Ditch lights and sirens for fall-alarm calls

    Prehospital Emergency Care · 19 Oct 2026 · Cohort · Hypothesis-generating

    Before-after study switching medical-alert (PERS) activations to non-lights-and-siren dispatch — L&S response fell from 98% to 12% of calls and response-phase L&S hours dropped 86%, with only 0.2% of calls later needing an upgrade to L&S transport.

  • Why patients decline hypoglycaemia call-back programs

    Diabetes, Obesity, and Cardiometabolic CARE · 19 Oct 2026 · Qualitative · Context only

    Qualitative interviews with adults who declined a community paramedicine hypoglycaemia-prevention program found they normalised severe episodes within their own management routines — time constraints (56%), perceived lack of benefit (25%) and privacy concerns about home visits (25%) kept them out.

  • Mobile ECPR team cannulates on scene

    Resuscitation Plus · 19 Oct 2026 · Other · Context only

    Protocol for a single-arm pilot dispatching a mobile ECPR team to cannulate refractory OHCA patients at the scene, aiming to cut collapse-to-flow from the ~60 minutes typical of in-hospital ECPR by at least 15 minutes.

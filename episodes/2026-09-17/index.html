Papers in this episode

  • Fluids in traumatic cardiac arrest: rat model

    Resuscitation Plus · 17 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Rat model of traumatic cardiac arrest (30% haemorrhage plus asphyxia) comparing four fluids after ROSC found 96-hour survival of 90% with whole blood, 80% with HES, 60% with lactated Ringer's and just 30% with normal saline — crystalloids prolonged survival without improving haemodynamics.

  • LLMs unreliable for cath-lab activation ECGs

    Heart & Lung · 17 Sep 2026 · Other · Context only

    Retrospective test of three multimodal LLMs against real cardiology cath-lab activation decisions on 615 prehospital ECGs found the best (Gemini, 95.3% sensitivity) had 9.4% specificity, and the standard ECG machine algorithm beat all three LLMs overall — general-purpose LLMs aren't reliable for STEMI pathway activation.

  • Best height for standing compressions

    Resuscitation Plus · 17 Sep 2026 · Simulation · Hypothesis-generating

    Simulation study with 30 participants compressing at seven working heights found knee-height bed or stretcher position gave the best compressions (52.7 mm mean depth, 80.6% correct depth), with positions 15–20 cm above knee height significantly worse.

  • Hot debrief tool in metropolitan EDs

    Emergency Medicine Australasia · 17 Sep 2026 · Other · Hypothesis-generating

    Multi-site Australian quality improvement study of a structured hot debrief tool after critical events (46 debriefs across two metropolitan EDs) found 98% of attendees rated it psychologically safe and 59 of 60 found it useful, with themes surfacing system issues like medication dosing in rare events and staff safety during behavioural emergencies.

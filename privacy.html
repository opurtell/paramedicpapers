Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Paramedic Papers is a free, non-commercial project run by one Australian paramedic. This page explains, in plain English, what the site stores about you, where it's kept, and how to get it back or delete it. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and there's no advertising.

Reading the site and podcast

  • No account is needed. Papers you save and your playback speed are kept in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Episode audio comes from audio.paramedicpapers.com, which is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, they see your IP address when your browser fetches a file.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so I can see roughly how many people use the site, which pages, from which countries and on what kind of device. It sets no cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't record who you are.
  • The list of other podcasts you can log as CPD comes from each podcast's public feed. We serve the episode details and artwork from this site, so browsing the list sends nothing to the podcast hosts.

The PD brief email

  • When you sign up we store your email address, the name you give (if any), and when you signed up. Cloudflare sends you a verification email, and the brief is sent through Cloudflare's email service.
  • Every issue has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes your address from the mailing list.

CPD tracking (when you sign in)

To keep a CPD record you sign in with Google or Microsoft. We only ask them for your name, email address and an account ID. We never see your password and get no access to your mail, files or contacts.

What's stored:

  • Your name, email address and sign-in provider ID.
  • Your CPD entries, learning goals and reflections, and your hours targets.
  • Your quiz attempts and scores.
  • For podcast episodes you log: the podcast's name, the episode title, its release date and a link to its page, alongside your other CPD data.
  • A sign-in session cookie. It's the only cookie the site sets.

It's stored in a Cloudflare D1 database in the Oceania region, with nightly backups in Cloudflare R2 storage. Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs for a few days so errors can be diagnosed.

Don't enter patient-identifying information. Reflections should be about what you learned and how it changes your practice. Leave out names, dates of birth, URNs, incident numbers, addresses, and any detail that could identify a patient.

Your record, your responsibility

  • The Paramedicine Board of Australia can audit your CPD, and you are responsible for keeping your own records. Export your portfolio at the end of each registration year (30 November) and keep it for 5 years. Don't rely on this site as your only copy.
  • You can download all your data (CSV, a printable portfolio, or the full JSON) at any time from the Account page.
  • Your CPD data is kept until you delete it. Deleting your account from the Account page removes your entries, goals, reflections and quiz attempts straight away. Backup copies age out within 30 days.

Contact

Questions, or want something removed? Email newsletter@paramedicpapers.com. It reaches me directly.
